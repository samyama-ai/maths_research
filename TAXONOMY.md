# Taxonomy — Maths Research Problems

10 topics, each representing a major field of mathematics. Each topic directory contains a `README.md` index and one `.md` file per problem/conjecture.

| # | Topic | Directory | Scope |
|---|-------|-----------|-------|
| 01 | Number Theory | [`01-number-theory`](./topics/01-number-theory/) | Distribution of primes, Diophantine equations, elliptic curves, modular forms, class field theory, arithmetic geometry |
| 02 | Algebra & Group Theory | [`02-algebra-group-theory`](./topics/02-algebra-group-theory/) | Representation theory, classification of groups, ring & module theory, homological algebra, associative & non-associative algebras |
| 03 | Algebraic & Differential Geometry | [`03-geometry`](./topics/03-geometry/) | Algebraic varieties, manifolds, symplectic geometry, Riemannian manifolds, curvature, discrete & convex geometry |
| 04 | Topology & Knot Theory | [`04-topology`](./topics/04-topology/) | Low-dimensional topology, homotopy groups, knot invariants, manifolds and cell complexes, K-theory |
| 05 | Real & Complex Analysis | [`05-analysis`](./topics/05-analysis/) | Fourier analysis, operator algebras, complex dynamics, functional analysis, measure theory |
| 06 | Partial Differential Equations | [`06-pdes`](./topics/06-pdes/) | Existence/uniqueness of solutions, regularity, Navier-Stokes smoothness, dispersive & wave equations |
| 07 | Combinatorics & Graph Theory | [`07-combinatorics`](./topics/07-combinatorics/) | Ramsey theory, extremal graph theory, enumerative & algebraic combinatorics, combinatorial designs, matroid theory |
| 08 | Mathematical Logic & Set Theory | [`08-logic-set-theory`](./topics/08-logic-set-theory/) | Model theory, computability, cardinal arithmetic, descriptive set theory, proof complexity, consistency/independence |
| 09 | Probability & Stochastic Processes | [`09-probability`](./topics/09-probability/) | Stochastic differential equations, random matrices, percolation theory, Schramm-Loewner evolution (SLE) |
| 10 | Theoretical Computer Science | [`10-theoretical-cs`](./topics/10-theoretical-cs/) | P vs NP, arithmetic complexity, cryptographic assumptions, quantum algorithms & information theory |

> The flat list of every problem is generated into [`INDEX.md`](./INDEX.md).
