# P-ideal Dichotomy Consistency

> **Topic:** Mathematical Logic & Set Theory · **ID:** `08-logic-set-theory/p-ideal-dichotomy-consistency` · **Status:** solved-recently

## 1. Problem Statement / Conjecture

The **P-ideal dichotomy (PID)** is the following statement about ideals of countable sets:

> For every set $S$ and every P-ideal $\mathcal{I} \subseteq [S]^{\le\omega}$, at least one of the following holds:
> 1. there is an uncountable $A \subseteq S$ with $[A]^{\le\omega} \subseteq \mathcal{I}$;
> 2. $S = \bigcup_{n<\omega} S_n$ where each $S_n$ is orthogonal to $\mathcal{I}$, i.e. $|S_n \cap I| < \aleph_0$ for every $I \in \mathcal{I}$.

The *consistency problem* asks: **is PID consistent with ZFC, and at what large-cardinal cost?** A complete answer has three parts: (a) a model of ZFC + PID; (b) a lower bound on consistency strength, i.e. inner models of ZFC + PID compute large cardinals; (c) an exact calibration matching (a) and (b).

Parts (a) and (b) are settled. PID is consistent relative to a supercompact cardinal (Todorcevic 2000: $\mathrm{PFA} \Rightarrow \mathrm{PID}$), and PID is *not* consistent relative to ZFC alone: it outright refutes $\square_\kappa$ for every uncountable $\kappa$, so PID implies $0^\sharp$ exists and much more. Part (c) — the exact strength, and whether PID decides $2^{\aleph_0}$ — remains open. The page is tagged `solved-recently` for the consistency question proper; the calibration questions are flagged open below.

## 2. Mathematical Foundations

Let $S$ be a set. An **ideal of countable sets** on $S$ is $\mathcal{I} \subseteq [S]^{\le\omega}$ closed under subsets and finite unions, containing $[S]^{<\omega}$. Write $A \subseteq^* B$ for $|A \setminus B| < \aleph_0$.

$\mathcal{I}$ is a **P-ideal** if it is countably directed modulo finite:
$$\forall \{A_n\}_{n<\omega} \subseteq \mathcal{I} \;\; \exists A \in \mathcal{I} \;\; \forall n \;\; A_n \subseteq^* A .$$

$X \subseteq S$ is **orthogonal to $\mathcal{I}$**, written $X \perp \mathcal{I}$, if $|X \cap I| < \aleph_0$ for all $I \in \mathcal{I}$. $A$ is **$\mathcal{I}$-large** (alternative (1)) if $[A]^{\le\omega} \subseteq \mathcal{I}$. The two alternatives are mutually exclusive whenever $|S| \ge \aleph_1$ and (1) holds with $A$ uncountable, since an uncountable $A$ cannot be covered by countably many finite traces.

For $S$ countable the statement is vacuous: $S = \bigcup_n \{s_n\}$ and each singleton is orthogonal to $\mathcal{I}$. The content lies in $|S| \ge \aleph_1$; by a standard reduction it suffices to treat $S = \omega_1$ and, for $\mathcal I$ on a countable base set, the *definable* case.

**Solecki's representation.** Every analytic P-ideal $\mathcal{I}$ on $\omega$ is an exhaustive ideal of a lower semicontinuous submeasure $\varphi : \mathcal{P}(\omega) \to [0,\infty]$:
$$\mathcal{I} = \mathrm{Exh}(\varphi) := \Big\{ A \subseteq \omega : \lim_{n\to\infty} \varphi\big(A \setminus n\big) = 0 \Big\}.$$
(Solecki, 1999.) Example: the density-zero ideal $\mathcal{Z} = \mathrm{Exh}(\varphi)$ with $\varphi(A) = \sup_n |A \cap [2^n,2^{n+1})| / 2^n$.

**Relevant axioms.** $\mathrm{PFA}$: every proper poset $P$ and family of $\aleph_1$ dense sets admits a filter meeting them all. $\square_\kappa$ (Jensen): a coherent sequence $\langle C_\alpha : \alpha \in \mathrm{Lim}(\kappa^+)\rangle$ with $\mathrm{otp}(C_\alpha)\le\kappa$ and coherence under limit points, with no thread. $\mathrm{SCH}$: $\kappa^{\mathrm{cf}\kappa} = \kappa^+$ for singular strong limit $\kappa$.

**Core facts (all ZFC theorems).**
- PID holds for every analytic P-ideal on a countable set (Todorcevic 2000, via Abraham–Todorcevic 1997 techniques).
- $\mathrm{PFA} \Rightarrow \mathrm{PID}$ (Todorcevic 2000): the forcing shooting an $\mathcal{I}$-large uncountable set is proper when alternative (2) fails.
- $\mathrm{PID} \Rightarrow \neg\square_\kappa$ for all uncountable $\kappa$ (Todorcevic 2000).
- $\mathrm{PID} \Rightarrow \mathrm{SCH}$ (Viale, 2006/2008 covering-property argument).

## 3. History & State of the Art (SOTA)

- **1997.** Abraham and Todorcevic, *Partition properties of $\omega_1$ compatible with CH*, isolate a dichotomy for P-ideals generated by $\aleph_1$ many sets and prove it consistent **with CH** by a countable-support iteration of totally proper forcings.
- **2000.** Todorcevic, *A dichotomy for P-ideals of countable sets*, states PID in full generality, proves it for analytic P-ideals in ZFC, derives it from PFA, and deduces $\neg\square_\kappa$, hence relative consistency exactly between "$0^\sharp$ exists" and "supercompact".
- **2005.** Steel proves $\mathrm{PFA} \Rightarrow \mathrm{AD}^{L(\mathbb{R})}$ by core model induction, with global failure of square as a central input — the technique that any sharp PID lower bound must use.
- **2006–2008.** Viale shows $\mathrm{PFA} \Rightarrow \mathrm{SCH}$ and then that the covering argument needs only PID; so PID settles a classical cardinal-arithmetic question outright.
- **2011.** Viale–Weiß show that obtaining PFA by a proper forcing iteration over a set-sized ground model requires a supercompact, closing the "cheap upper bound" route for PFA-style axioms.
- **2012–2014.** Todorcevic–Torres Pérez study PID alongside Rado's Conjecture and Chang-type reflection; Todorcevic's *Notes on Forcing Axioms* gives the canonical modern exposition.

SOTA: consistency strength of PID is trapped in the interval $[\,\text{“}\mathrm{AD}^{L(\mathbb R)}\text{-level lower bounds via core model induction”},\ \text{supercompact}\,]$, with the upper bound not known to be improvable.

## 4. Partial Results / Verified Cases

- **All analytic (indeed $\Sigma^1_1$) P-ideals on a countable base set:** dichotomy provable in ZFC, no extra axioms, using Solecki's $\mathrm{Exh}(\varphi)$ representation.
- **All ideals on a countable $S$:** trivially true (alternative (2)).
- **P-ideals with a generating family of size $\aleph_1$:** consistent with CH (Abraham–Todorcevic 1997); so the restricted dichotomy does not disturb the continuum.
- **Under PFA, MM, and their standard strengthenings:** PID holds in full; hence it holds in every model obtained by the usual length-supercompact countable-support iteration.
- **Consequences verified from PID alone:** $\neg\square_\kappa$ for all $\kappa \ge \aleph_1$; $\mathrm{SCH}$; $2^{\aleph_0} \ge \aleph_2$ is *not* known but $\mathfrak{b} \ne \aleph_1$-type consequences follow in the presence of standard companions; failure of PID in $L$, in $L[U]$, and in any model of global square.
- **Independence data points:** PID does not imply $\mathrm{MA}_{\aleph_1}$; PID is compatible with the existence of Suslin trees in known constructions *(frontier — verify)*.

## 5. Principal Obstacles

1. **Lower bound side — no canonical inner model.** Proving PID needs more than $0^\sharp$ requires core model induction: build $K^c$-style models, show they compute $\square$, contradict PID. Core model theory is only fully developed below a Woodin limit of Woodins, so the induction stalls exactly where the interesting strength (supercompactness) lives.
2. **Upper bound side — PID has no known "small" forcing.** The only known way to force PID is the PFA-style iteration, which by Viale–Weiß consumes a supercompact. There is no known iteration theorem tailored to P-ideals alone that avoids full properness preservation.
3. **The uncountable base set breaks definability.** Analytic P-ideals on $\omega$ yield to descriptive set theory (submeasures, Baire category on $2^\omega$). For $\mathcal{I} \subseteq [\omega_1]^{\le\omega}$ there is no Polish topology, so Solecki's exhaustivity theorem, Baire-category arguments, and Borel determinacy all fail to apply.
4. **Alternative (2) is a $\sigma$-decomposition, not a reflection statement.** Standard elementary-submodel reflection produces one countable approximation, not a countable partition; gluing the pieces needs a fusion argument whose properness is exactly what the PFA iteration supplies.
5. **CH tension.** The Abraham–Todorcevic totally proper iteration handles $\aleph_1$-generated ideals under CH, but a P-ideal generated by $\mathfrak{c} > \aleph_1$ sets escapes the bookkeeping; whether full PID is compatible with CH is unresolved.

## 6. The Gap

Proven: (i) $\mathrm{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC} + \text{supercompact}) \Rightarrow \mathrm{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC} + \mathrm{PID})$; (ii) $\mathrm{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC}+\mathrm{PID}) \Rightarrow \mathrm{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC} + \text{“}0^\sharp \text{ exists”})$ and, by Schimmerling–Steel-type maximality arguments, considerably more.

Not proven: any statement of the form
$$\mathrm{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC}+\mathrm{PID}) \Longleftrightarrow \mathrm{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC}+\Phi)$$
for a natural large-cardinal hypothesis $\Phi$. The precise missing steps: (a) a core model induction showing $\mathrm{PID} \Rightarrow \mathrm{AD}^{L(\mathbb{R})}$ without the extra PFA consequences Steel uses; (b) a forcing construction producing PID from something strictly weaker than supercompact — e.g. from a strongly compact or from a proper class of Woodins; (c) settling Todorcevic's question **does $\mathrm{PID}$ imply $2^{\aleph_0} \le \aleph_2$?**, and the dual question of $\mathrm{PID} + \mathrm{CH}$.

## 7. Current Research (as of June 2026)

- **Core model induction.** Groups around Münster (Schindler and collaborators) and Berkeley/UC Irvine continue pushing $\mathrm{AD}^{L(\mathbb R)}$-and-beyond lower bounds from fragments of forcing axioms; extending these to PID alone is an announced target *(frontier — verify)*.
- **Guessing models and $\mathrm{ISP}$.** Viale's Torino school studies guessing-model principles as strength-calibrated surrogates for PFA; whether PID follows from a guessing-model axiom of strictly sub-supercompact strength is actively studied *(frontier — verify)*.
- **PID and cardinal arithmetic.** Continued analysis of PID's covering consequences beyond SCH, including PID + weak reflection at $\aleph_2$ and interaction with Rado's Conjecture (Todorcevic, Torres Pérez, and successors).
- **Definable versions.** Extending the ZFC theorem from analytic to universally Baire P-ideals on $\omega$ under determinacy hypotheses *(frontier — verify)*.

## 8. Future Work

- Isolate a *P-ideal-specific* iteration theorem: an iterable class of posets that shoots $\mathcal{I}$-large sets but is preserved under a countable support iteration of length $<$ the first supercompact.
- Run the core model induction directly from $\neg\square_\kappa$ (global) plus $\mathrm{SCH}$ — both PID consequences — to see how far the strength climbs without further axioms.
- Decide $\mathrm{PID} + \mathrm{CH}$: either extend the Abraham–Todorcevic totally proper iteration to arbitrarily generated P-ideals, or find a $\mathrm{CH}$-refuting consequence of PID.
- Determine whether PID implies $\mathfrak{c} \le \aleph_2$ (Todorcevic's question), possibly by a Baumgartner-style side-condition argument.
- Map the implication lattice: PID vs. OCA, vs. Rado's Conjecture, vs. $\mathrm{MRP}$ — which pairs are provably non-implying, and which share consistency strength.

## 10. Worked Example / Concrete Special Case

**A P-ideal on $\omega_1$ falling on side (2).** Fix a partition $\omega_1 = \bigcup_{n<\omega} S_n$ into uncountable pieces and set
$$\mathcal{I} = \big\{ A \in [\omega_1]^{\le\omega} : |A \cap S_n| < \aleph_0 \ \text{ for all } n<\omega \big\}.$$

*It is an ideal:* closure under subsets and finite unions is immediate, and all finite sets belong.

*It is a P-ideal:* given $A_k \in \mathcal{I}$ ($k<\omega$), put
$$A \;=\; \bigcup_{k<\omega} \Big( A_k \setminus \bigcup_{n<k} S_n \Big).$$
Then $A_k \setminus A \subseteq A_k \cap \bigcup_{n<k} S_n$, a finite union of finite sets, so $A_k \subseteq^* A$. And $A \cap S_n \subseteq \bigcup_{k \le n} (A_k \cap S_n)$ is finite, so $A \in \mathcal{I}$.

*Which alternative?* Alternative (1) fails: if $A \subseteq \omega_1$ is uncountable then some $A \cap S_n$ is uncountable, so $A$ has a countably infinite subset inside $S_n$, which is not in $\mathcal{I}$; hence $[A]^{\le\omega} \not\subseteq \mathcal{I}$. Alternative (2) holds by construction: each $S_n \perp \mathcal{I}$, since $|S_n \cap I| < \aleph_0$ for every $I \in \mathcal{I}$ by definition of $\mathcal{I}$.

**Where the axiom bites.** Replace the fixed partition by an ideal read off a $\square_{\aleph_1}$-sequence $\langle C_\alpha : \alpha \in \mathrm{Lim}(\aleph_2)\rangle$: coherence produces a P-ideal on $\aleph_2$ for which the $\sigma$-decomposition of (2) contradicts non-threadedness, while (1) contradicts $\mathrm{otp}(C_\alpha)\le\aleph_1$ coherence. That is the shape of Todorcevic's proof that $\mathrm{PID} \Rightarrow \neg\square_\kappa$. Consequently, in $L$ — where $\square_\kappa$ holds for all $\kappa$ — PID **fails**, so no model of PID is inner-model-cheap: the analytic case is free in ZFC, the $\omega_1$-partition case is free, but the coherent case costs large cardinals.
