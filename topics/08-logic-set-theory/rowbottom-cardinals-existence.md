# Rowbottom Cardinals Existence

> **Topic:** Mathematical Logic & Set Theory · **ID:** `08-logic-set-theory/rowbottom-cardinals-existence` · **Status:** partially-solved

## 1. Problem Statement / Conjecture

A Rowbottom cardinal is an uncountable cardinal $\kappa$ such that every colouring of the finite subsets of $\kappa$ by fewer than $\kappa$ colours has a set of size $\kappa$ on which only countably many colours appear. Existence is not provable in ZFC (it fails in $L$), so the questions are all relative:

1. **Consistency strength.** What is the exact consistency strength of "there is a Rowbottom cardinal"? Known bounds are: below "there is a measurable cardinal" and above "there is a Ramsey cardinal" in the core-model sense. Closing this gap — in particular deciding whether $\mathrm{Con}(\exists\,\text{Ramsey}) \Rightarrow \mathrm{Con}(\exists\,\text{Rowbottom})$ — is open.
2. **Structure.** Must a *regular* Rowbottom cardinal be Ramsey, or at least measurable in an inner model of the same strength? Must it be inaccessible?
3. **Location.** Which specific cardinals can be Rowbottom? $\aleph_\omega$ can (from a measurable); can $\aleph_1$? Can a successor of a regular cardinal?

A complete solution to (1) means an equiconsistency: a forcing producing a Rowbottom cardinal from hypothesis $H$, plus a core-model argument showing a Rowbottom cardinal yields an inner model for $H$.

## 2. Mathematical Foundations

Write $[X]^n$ for the $n$-element subsets of $X$ and $[X]^{<\omega}=\bigcup_{n<\omega}[X]^n$.

**Partition notation.** For cardinals $\kappa,\mu,\lambda$,
$$\kappa \longrightarrow [\kappa]^{<\omega}_{\mu,<\lambda}$$
means: for every $f:[\kappa]^{<\omega}\to\mu$ there is $H\subseteq\kappa$ with $|H|=\kappa$ and
$$\bigl|\,f\,''[H]^{<\omega}\,\bigr| < \lambda .$$

**Definition (Rowbottom).** $\kappa>\omega$ is *Rowbottom* iff
$$\forall \mu<\kappa \quad \kappa \longrightarrow [\kappa]^{<\omega}_{\mu,<\omega_1}.$$
More generally $\kappa$ is *$\lambda$-Rowbottom* if $\kappa\to[\kappa]^{<\omega}_{\mu,<\lambda}$ for all $\mu<\kappa$; Rowbottom $=$ $\omega_1$-Rowbottom.

**Model-theoretic form.** $\kappa$ is Rowbottom iff for every structure $\mathfrak{A}=\langle \kappa,\dots\rangle$ in a countable language and every $\mu<\kappa$ with $\mu$ uncountable, there is $\mathfrak{B}\prec\mathfrak{A}$ with $|B|=\kappa$ and $|B\cap\mu|=\omega$. This is the "Rowbottom submodel" reading and is the form used in applications.

**Definition (Jónsson).** $\kappa$ is *Jónsson* iff every structure on $\kappa$ in a countable language has a proper elementary substructure of size $\kappa$; equivalently $\kappa\to[\kappa]^{<\omega}_\kappa$. Trivially
$$\kappa \text{ Rowbottom} \;\Longrightarrow\; \kappa \text{ Jónsson}.$$

**Rowbottom filter.** A $\kappa$-complete uniform filter $F$ on $\kappa$ is a *Rowbottom filter* if for every $\mu<\kappa$ and $f:[\kappa]^{<\omega}\to\mu$ there is $H\in F$ with $|f\,''[H]^{<\omega}|\le\omega$. Every measurable carries one (its normal ultrafilter); the interest is in singular $\kappa$ carrying one.

**Ambient hierarchy.** With $\kappa\to(\kappa)^{<\omega}_2$ defining *Ramsey* and $\kappa\to(\alpha)^{<\omega}_2$ for all $\alpha<\kappa$ defining the Erdős cardinal $\kappa(\alpha)$:
$$\text{measurable}\;\Rightarrow\;\text{Ramsey}\;\Rightarrow\;\text{Rowbottom}\;\Rightarrow\;\text{Jónsson}\;\Rightarrow\; 0^{\\\\#}\text{ exists}.$$
None of the last three implications is known to reverse in consistency strength.

## 3. History & State of the Art (SOTA)

- **1964–71.** Frederick Rowbottom's Wisconsin thesis, published as *Some strong axioms of infinity incompatible with the axiom of constructibility* (Ann. Math. Logic 3, 1971), introduces the partition property and shows a measurable cardinal implies $\kappa\to[\kappa]^{<\omega}_{\mu,<\omega_1}$, hence that these cardinals contradict $V=L$.
- **1966.** Erdős–Hajnal, *On a problem of B. Jónsson* (Bull. Acad. Polon. Sci.), fixes the Jónsson framework and its links to partition relations.
- **1970.** Kunen's iterated-ultrapower techniques and Prikry's *Changing measurable into accessible cardinals* (Dissertationes Math. 68) give the first *singular* Rowbottom cardinals: Prikry forcing over a measurable $\kappa$ followed by a Lévy collapse makes $\kappa=\aleph_\omega$ carry a Rowbottom filter generated by tail sets.
- **1980s.** Devlin (*Constructibility*, 1984) and Dodd–Jensen (*The core model*, Ann. Pure Appl. Logic 20, 1981) put lower bounds in place: covering over $K^{DJ}$ excludes Jónsson cardinals, so a Rowbottom cardinal gives an inner model with a measurable. Donder–Koepke (Ann. Pure Appl. Logic 25, 1983) push accessible Jónsson cardinals and the weak Chang conjecture to strictly higher strength.
- **1999.** Mitchell, *Jónsson cardinals, Erdős cardinals and the core model* (J. Symbolic Logic 64): below the relevant anti-large-cardinal hypothesis, a Jónsson (hence Rowbottom) cardinal is Ramsey in $K$. This is the current SOTA lower bound and the reason the problem is now stated as "Jónsson/Rowbottom $=$ Ramsey?"
- **Determinacy side.** Under AD, $\aleph_1$ and $\aleph_2$ are Rowbottom with the club filter (Kleinberg, *Infinitary combinatorics and the axiom of determinateness*, LNM 612, 1977) — so consistently (relative to AD) a successor cardinal is Rowbottom, but not in a ZFC + AC model of that kind.

## 4. Partial Results / Verified Cases

- **Measurable $\Rightarrow$ Rowbottom** (Rowbottom 1971): with the normal ultrafilter as Rowbottom filter. Also **Ramsey $\Rightarrow$ Rowbottom**, since $\kappa\to(\kappa)^{<\omega}_2$ gives homogeneous sets of size $\kappa$ for each arity.
- **Singular case, cofinality $\omega$:** relative to one measurable, $\aleph_\omega$ can be Rowbottom (Prikry 1970 + Lévy collapse). Hence "least Rowbottom cardinal is singular" is consistent; Rowbottom does **not** imply regular, inaccessible, or Ramsey in general.
- **Uncountable cofinality:** a Jónsson cardinal of uncountable cofinality is a limit of Jónsson-type structure and is heavily constrained; Tryba (*On Jónsson cardinals with uncountable cofinality*, Israel J. Math. 49, 1984) and Shelah's pcf machinery (*Cardinal Arithmetic*, OUP 1994) rule out large classes, e.g. Jónsson cardinals of the form $\aleph_{\alpha}$ with $\alpha<\aleph_\alpha$ under pcf hypotheses.
- **Lower bounds:** Rowbottom $\Rightarrow 0^{\\\\#}$; accessible Rowbottom $\Rightarrow$ inner model with a measurable (Donder–Koepke 1983); Rowbottom $\Rightarrow$ $\kappa$ Ramsey in $K$ under Mitchell's hypothesis (1999).
- **$\lambda$-Rowbottom for $\lambda>\omega_1$:** for $\lambda=\kappa$ this degenerates to Jónsson; the strict hierarchy $\omega_1$-Rowbottom $\Rightarrow$ $\lambda$-Rowbottom for $\omega_1\le\lambda$ is immediate, and no reversal is known at any level.
- **Determinacy models:** $\aleph_1,\aleph_2$ Rowbottom under AD (Kleinberg 1977); $\aleph_{\omega_1}$-type analogues under AD$^+$.

## 5. Principal Obstacles

- **No ultrafilter to iterate.** All classical machinery for measurables (ultrapowers, iterations, indiscernibles) needs a $\kappa$-complete *ultra*filter. A Rowbottom filter need not be an ultrafilter and need not be normal, so no elementary embedding is available; one cannot run the Kunen-style argument to derive $0^{\\\\#}$-like objects at higher levels.
- **Singularity breaks reflection.** Since $\kappa$ may be singular of cofinality $\omega$ (the $\aleph_\omega$ model), any proof must survive without regularity: no diagonal intersections of length $\kappa$, no closed unbounded filter that is $\kappa$-complete, no stationary-set reflection.
- **Core-model covering is the only lower-bound tool, and it stalls.** Mitchell's argument shows Jónsson $\Rightarrow$ Ramsey in $K$, but only below the level where $K$ is currently constructed with full covering (roughly below a Woodin cardinal, and in Mitchell's paper below strength where $K$ is fine-structural and rigid enough). Above that, "$K$ exists" is itself the open problem, so the lower bound cannot be improved by the same route.
- **No forcing construction from below.** There is no known forcing that manufactures a Rowbottom cardinal from a Ramsey cardinal. Every known model of "$\exists$ Rowbottom" is obtained from a measurable (or stronger), because the partition property for *all* finite arities simultaneously is preserved only by very tame forcings (Prikry-type, with the Prikry property).
- **Countability of the colour-set is fragile.** The bound "$\le\omega$ colours" is not a large-cardinal-invariant statement: it is not preserved by collapses that change $\omega_1$, and is not directly absolute between $V$ and inner models, blocking condensation arguments.

## 6. The Gap

Proven: $\text{measurable} \Rightarrow \text{Rowbottom} \Rightarrow \text{Jónsson} \Rightarrow \text{Ramsey in } K$. The gap is the interval
$$\mathrm{Con}(\exists\,\text{Ramsey}) \;\;\le?\;\; \mathrm{Con}(\exists\,\text{Rowbottom}) \;\;\le\;\; \mathrm{Con}(\exists\,\text{measurable}),$$
where the left inequality is exactly what is missing. Crossing it needs one of two steps:

- **Upward:** a forcing (or inner-model) construction producing $\kappa\to[\kappa]^{<\omega}_{\mu,<\omega_1}$ for all $\mu<\kappa$ starting only from a Ramsey cardinal, or from an Erdős cardinal $\kappa(\alpha)$ — no candidate forcing is known that adds the required homogeneous sets for all arities at once.
- **Downward:** a proof that a Rowbottom cardinal yields an inner model with a measurable *of the same cardinal*, i.e. upgrading Mitchell's "Ramsey in $K$" to "measurable in $K$", which would require a covering-style argument for $K$ at Mitchell orders currently out of reach.

For the local questions the gap is sharper: $\aleph_\omega$ Rowbottom is known from one measurable and known to imply inner models with many measurables (Donder–Koepke direction), but no equiconsistency is established.

## 7. Current Research (as of June 2026)

- **Core-model induction and Jónsson cardinals.** Groups in Münster (Schindler and collaborators) and Bonn continue to extend $K$-existence to higher hypotheses; each extension mechanically improves the lower bound for Rowbottom/Jónsson. *(frontier — verify)*
- **Successors of singulars.** Work descending from Donder–Levinski (*Some principles related to Chang's conjecture*, Ann. Pure Appl. Logic 45, 1989) and continued by Eskew–Hayut on global Chang's Conjecture connects "$\aleph_{\omega+1}$ Jónsson" to Chang-type transfer principles; a Rowbottom successor of a singular remains unconstructed. *(frontier — verify)*
- **pcf-theoretic exclusions.** Shelah-style arguments continue to prune candidate singular Jónsson/Rowbottom cardinals of uncountable cofinality; the target is to show every Jónsson cardinal is either a limit of measurables in $K$ or has cofinality $\omega$.
- **Determinacy models.** Under AD$^+$, Rowbottom-type filters at $\aleph_1,\aleph_2$ and at odd projective ordinals are studied as combinatorial consequences of strong partition properties (Jackson's programme).

## 8. Future Work

- Build the missing forcing: a Prikry-like iteration over an Erdős or Ramsey cardinal designed to produce Rowbottom filters, testing whether the Prikry property suffices to preserve all-arity partition relations.
- Settle the regular case first: prove or refute "every regular Rowbottom cardinal is Ramsey". A regular Rowbottom cardinal is Jónsson and, by Mitchell, Ramsey in $K$; showing it is Ramsey in $V$ would collapse the structural question.
- Determine whether $\aleph_1$ can be Rowbottom under ZFC + AC (Kleinberg's AD result shows the combinatorics is coherent without choice).
- Isolate a Rowbottom-filter analogue of normality that supports an iteration, giving an embedding-based lower-bound argument independent of core models.

## 10. Worked Example / Concrete Special Case

**Claim.** If $\kappa$ is measurable with normal ultrafilter $U$, then $\kappa$ is Rowbottom.

*Step 1 — a concrete arity-2 case.* Fix $\mu<\kappa$ and a partition $\kappa=\bigcup_{i<\mu}S_i$ into disjoint pieces. Define
$$f(\{\alpha,\beta\}) = i \quad\text{where } \alpha<\beta \text{ and } \beta\in S_i .$$
Since $U$ is a $\kappa$-complete ultrafilter and $\mu<\kappa$, exactly one piece is large: there is a unique $i_0<\mu$ with $S_{i_0}\in U$. Put $H=S_{i_0}$. Then $|H|=\kappa$ and for every $\{\alpha,\beta\}\in[H]^2$ we get $f(\{\alpha,\beta\})=i_0$, so
$$\bigl|f\,''[H]^2\bigr| = 1 .$$

*Step 2 — arbitrary arity.* By Rowbottom's partition theorem for measurables, for each $n<\omega$ and each $g:[\kappa]^n\to\mu$ with $\mu<\kappa$ there is $H_g\in U$ with $|g\,''[H_g]^n|=1$. (Proof sketch for $n=2$ and $\mu=2$: the Ramsey property $\kappa\to(\kappa)^2_2$ holds for measurables by taking $H=\{\alpha : \{\beta>\alpha : f(\{\alpha,\beta\})=0\}\in U\}$ or its complement, whichever lies in $U$, then thinning by a diagonal intersection using normality of $U$.)

*Step 3 — assemble.* Given $f:[\kappa]^{<\omega}\to\mu$, let $f_n = f\restriction[\kappa]^n$ and choose $H_n\in U$ homogeneous for $f_n$ with constant value $c_n<\mu$. Since $U$ is $\kappa$-complete and $\omega<\kappa$,
$$H=\bigcap_{n<\omega}H_n \in U, \qquad |H|=\kappa,$$
and every finite subset of $H$ of size $n$ receives colour $c_n$. Hence
$$f\,''[H]^{<\omega} \subseteq \{c_n : n<\omega\}, \qquad \bigl|f\,''[H]^{<\omega}\bigr| \le \omega < \omega_1 .$$
So $\kappa\to[\kappa]^{<\omega}_{\mu,<\omega_1}$ for every $\mu<\kappa$: $\kappa$ is Rowbottom, with $U$ itself a Rowbottom filter. $\square$

**Why this does not settle the problem.** Every step used that $U$ is an *ultra*filter (Step 1: exactly one $S_i$ is large) and $\kappa$-complete (Step 3). A Ramsey cardinal has homogeneous sets but no ultrafilter on all of $P(\kappa)$; a Prikry-generic $\aleph_\omega$ has a Rowbottom filter but is singular and carries no $\kappa$-complete ultrafilter. The open problem is precisely whether the conclusion can be reached without the hypothesis used here.
