# Radin Forcing Generalization

> **Topic:** Mathematical Logic & Set Theory · **ID:** `08-logic-set-theory/radin-forcing-generalization` · **Status:** partially-solved

## 1. Problem Statement / Conjecture

Radin forcing $\mathbb{R}_u$ adds a closed unbounded subset $C_u \subseteq \kappa$ to a large cardinal $\kappa$ while preserving cardinals and, for long enough measure sequences $u$, preserving large-cardinal properties of $\kappa$ itself. It uniformly generalizes Prikry forcing (measure sequence of length $1$) and Magidor forcing (length a limit ordinal $<\kappa$). The **Radin forcing generalization problem** is the cluster of open questions asking how far this machinery extends beyond coherent sequences of *normal measures on $\kappa$*:

1. **(Supercompact/extender generalization.)** Is there a Radin-type forcing built from sequences of **supercompactness measures** or **$(\kappa,\lambda)$-extenders** that simultaneously (i) adds a generic club through $\kappa$, (ii) blows up $2^\kappa$ to any prescribed $\lambda$, (iii) preserves all cardinals, and (iv) preserves a prescribed degree of supercompactness of $\kappa$ in the extension?
2. **(Genericity characterization.)** Is there a full **Mathias-style criterion** — "$C$ is $\mathbb{R}_u$-generic over $V$ iff $C$ almost-diagonalizes every set in the generated filter" — for Radin forcing at the extender-based and supercompact levels, matching the clean criterion available for Prikry forcing?
3. **(Optimal strength.)** What is the exact consistency strength of the configurations currently obtained *only* via Radin forcing from supercompactness — most prominently the Foreman–Woodin model in which $\mathsf{GCH}$ fails at every infinite cardinal?
4. **(Iterability.)** Which Radin-type forcings admit a Magidor-style iteration with a Prikry property, so that the club-adding can be performed class-many times without collapsing cardinals?

A complete solution to any part means either an explicit forcing construction with a verified Prikry property, cardinal-preservation and preservation theorem, or a consistency-strength lower bound (typically a core-model or covering argument) matching the known upper bound.

## 2. Mathematical Foundations

**Measure sequences.** Let $j : V \to M$ be an elementary embedding with critical point $\kappa$, $M$ transitive. Define by recursion the *measure sequence*
$$u = \langle \kappa \rangle ^\frown \langle U_\alpha : \alpha < \ell(u) \rangle, \qquad U_\alpha = \{\, X \subseteq V_\kappa : u \restriction \alpha \in j(X) \,\},$$
where the recursion continues as long as $u\restriction\alpha \in M$. Each $U_\alpha$ is a $\kappa$-complete ultrafilter on $V_\kappa$ concentrating on measure sequences of length $<\alpha$; $U_0$ is (essentially) a normal measure on $\kappa$. The **length** $\ell(u)$ is bounded by the degree of strongness of $j$: $\ell(u) \le (2^{2^\kappa})^+$, and $\ell(u)\ge 2$ already requires $\kappa$ to have Mitchell order $o(\kappa) \ge 1$.

Write $F(u) = \bigcap_{\alpha<\ell(u)} U_\alpha$ for the filter generated by the sequence, and $\kappa(u) = u(0)$ for the underlying ordinal.

**The forcing.** A condition in $\mathbb{R}_u$ is a finite sequence
$$p = \langle (u_0, A_0), (u_1, A_1), \dots, (u_{n-1},A_{n-1}), (u, A) \rangle$$
with $\kappa(u_0) < \cdots < \kappa(u_{n-1}) < \kappa$, each $u_i$ a measure sequence, $A_i \in F(u_i)$, $A \in F(u)$, and coherence: each $u_i$ appears in the constraint sets of later entries. Extension $q \le p$ allows shrinking the $A_i$ and inserting new pairs $(v, B)$ with $v \in A_i$ for the appropriate $i$. The *direct (Prikry) extension* $q \le^* p$ shrinks measure-one sets only.

**Prikry property.** For every statement $\sigma$ of the forcing language and every $p$, there is $q \le^* p$ deciding $\sigma$. Together with $\kappa^+$-cc and the fact that $(\mathbb{R}_u, \le^*)$ is $\kappa$-closed, this yields:

$$\mathbb{R}_u \text{ adds no bounded subsets of } \kappa \text{ and preserves all cardinals } \le \kappa \text{ and } \ge \kappa^+ .$$

**Generic club.** If $G$ is generic, $C_u = \{\kappa(v) : v \text{ appears in some } p\in G\}$ is club in $\kappa$, and every $\lambda \in C_u$ is (in $V$) a measurable cardinal carrying a measure sequence. The order type of $C_u$ tracks $\ell(u)$:
$$\ell(u)=1 \Rightarrow \operatorname{ot}(C_u)=\omega; \qquad \operatorname{cf}(\ell(u)) > \omega \Rightarrow \kappa \text{ remains regular (indeed Mahlo)}.$$

**Preservation.** For sufficiently long sequences — canonically, $u$ derived from a $j$ with $V_{\kappa+2}\subseteq M$ — $\kappa$ remains measurable in $V[G]$. This is the feature that has no analogue in Prikry or Magidor forcing, and it is what makes Radin forcing the tool of choice for iterated/global constructions.

## 3. History & State of the Art (SOTA)

- **1970.** Prikry: change $\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)$ to $\omega$ from a measurable, adding no bounded subsets.
- **1978.** Magidor: change $\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)$ to any regular $\delta<\kappa$ using a coherent $\lhd$-increasing sequence of measures; introduces the Mitchell-order-driven design.
- **1982.** Lon Radin's thesis work, *Adding closed cofinal sequences to large cardinals*, unifies both and adds the crucial new phenomenon: for long measure sequences the generic club is added **without** singularizing $\kappa$, and $\kappa$ can retain measurability, weak compactness or (with more) supercompactness.
- **1991.** Foreman–Woodin use Radin forcing over a supercompact to build the first model where $\mathsf{GCH}$ fails at *every* infinite cardinal — still the canonical application.
- **1992.** Cummings: $\mathsf{GCH}$ at successors, failing at limits, via a Radin-style construction.
- **2003.** Merimovich, *Extender-based Radin forcing*: replaces measures by extenders, so that $2^\kappa$ can be pushed to $\lambda$ *while* $\kappa$ stays regular and a club is added. This is the single largest step of the generalization program.
- **2007.** Krueger, *Radin forcing and its iterations*: systematic preservation and iteration theory.
- **2010.** Gitik's *Prikry-type forcings* and Cummings' *Iterated forcing and elementary embeddings* (both Handbook of Set Theory) fix the modern presentation and list the open problems.
- **2010s–2020s.** Merimovich's supercompact extender-based Prikry/Magidor–Radin forcings; Ben-Neria–Unger apply homogeneous Prikry/Radin-type forcings to $\mathrm{HOD}$; Radin forcing enters the tree-property and stationary-reflection literature.

## 4. Partial Results / Verified Cases

| Parameter regime | Status |
|---|---|
| $\ell(u)=1$ (one normal measure) | Solved: Prikry forcing; $\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)=\omega$, no new bounded subsets |
| $\ell(u)=\delta$ limit, $\delta<\kappa$, $o(\kappa)\ge\delta$ | Solved: Magidor forcing; $\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)=\operatorname{cf}(\delta)$ |
| $\operatorname{cf}(\ell(u))>\omega$ | Solved (Radin): $\kappa$ stays regular, inaccessible, Mahlo |
| $u$ derived from $j$ with $V_{\kappa+2}\subseteq M$ | Solved (Radin): $\kappa$ measurable in $V[G]$ |
| $u$ from a supercompactness embedding | Solved (Foreman–Woodin 1991): global failure of $\mathsf{GCH}$ |
| $(\kappa,\lambda)$-extender sequences, $\kappa$ regular in $V[G]$, $2^\kappa=\lambda$ | Solved (Merimovich 2003) |
| Iterations of length $\le$ the least inaccessible above | Largely solved (Krueger 2007) |
| Supercompact measure sequences with full Mathias criterion | **Open** |
| Lower bound for "$\mathsf{GCH}$ fails everywhere" | **Open** (gap: strong-ish lower bounds vs. supercompact upper bound) |
| Radin club $+$ tree property at $\kappa^+$ | **Open in general** |

Concrete verified instance: from $o(\kappa)=\kappa^{++}$ one gets a Radin generic club consisting of former measurables with $\kappa$ still measurable — and by Mitchell's core-model analysis $o(\kappa)\ge\kappa^{++}$ is *necessary* for the corresponding failure of $\mathsf{SCH}$-type configurations, so this regime is strength-optimal.

## 5. Principal Obstacles

- **Coherence breaks for supercompactness measures.** The recursion defining $u$ needs $u\restriction\alpha \in M$. For measures on $P_\kappa\lambda$ the objects to be captured live at $\lambda$, and the natural sequences fail to be $\lhd$-coherent; there is no canonical well-order of the "supercompact measure sequences" analogous to the Mitchell order.
- **Loss of normality/niceness under extenders.** Extender-based conditions carry *domains* (partial functions), so the natural ordering is not $\kappa$-closed on the nose; proving the Prikry property requires delicate "fusion with domain growth", and each new generalization needs its own combinatorial lemma rather than an off-the-shelf argument.
- **No Mathias criterion at higher levels.** For Prikry forcing, genericity is characterized by an almost-containment condition. At the Radin level the generic object is a club of *measure sequences*, and the induced filter is not $\sigma$-generated; the known criteria require extra hypotheses on $u$. Without such a criterion, quotient analysis and iteration arguments must be redone by hand.
- **Core model theory stops short.** Lower-bound technology (covering lemmas, $K^{DJ}$, and the more recent hod-mice machinery) is effective up to Woodin cardinals in restricted settings, but the Foreman–Woodin configuration is proved from supercompactness, far above where inner-model theory currently gives matching lower bounds. This is the same barrier that blocks progress on $\mathsf{SCH}$-style strength questions generally.
- **Interaction with $\square$ and scales.** Radin clubs tend to add very good scales and approachability, which is exactly what kills the tree property and stationary reflection at $\kappa^+$; disentangling the club-adding from the square-like consequences is not known to be possible in general.

## 6. The Gap

What is proved: a complete, robust theory for **measure sequences derived from strongness embeddings** (Radin), and an extender-based version giving control over $2^\kappa$ (Merimovich). What is asked: the same theory for **supercompactness-derived sequences with prescribed preservation**, and matching **lower bounds**.

The precise step: define, from $j:V\to M$ with $M^\lambda\subseteq M$, a hierarchy of measures $\langle U_\alpha : \alpha<\ell\rangle$ on $P_\kappa\lambda$-like objects which is (a) coherent — each $U_\alpha$ concentrates on shorter sequences of the same type — and (b) closed under the diagonal-intersection operation needed for the Prikry property. Every known attempt satisfies (a) or (b) but not both simultaneously past the first few levels. On the strength side, the gap is between "$o(\kappa)$ in the Mitchell hierarchy" (where lower bounds exist) and "supercompact" (where the constructions live).

## 7. Current Research (as of June 2026)

- **Merimovich's programme (Open University of Israel).** Supercompact extender-based Prikry/Magidor–Radin forcings; the running goal is a single forcing template parameterized by an arbitrary coherent system of extenders. *(frontier — verify)* Recent preprints claim Prikry-property proofs for supercompact extender-based Radin forcing with prescribed $2^\kappa$.
- **Gitik's school (Tel Aviv).** Precise strength of $\mathsf{SCH}$-failures and of "$\kappa$ regular with a Radin club and $2^\kappa$ large"; short-extender methods as an alternative to long measure sequences.
- **Ben-Neria, Hayut, Unger.** Homogeneity properties of Prikry/Radin-type forcings and consequences for $\mathrm{HOD}$; Mathias-style genericity criteria for Magidor iterations. *(frontier — verify)*
- **Neeman, Sinapova, Golshani and collaborators.** Combining Radin-type clubs with the tree property, stationary reflection, and failures of approachability at $\kappa^+$.
- **Inner-model side (Schindler, Sargsyan, Steel schools).** Hod-mice and the core-model induction, aimed at lower bounds for configurations currently obtained only from supercompactness.

## 8. Future Work

- Isolate the correct notion of "coherent supercompact measure sequence" — plausibly via *Mitchell order on $P_\kappa\lambda$-measures* restricted to a normal-like subclass — and verify the Prikry property for the resulting forcing.
- Prove a Mathias criterion for extender-based Radin forcing; this would immediately give quotient factorization and iteration theorems for free.
- Reduce the Foreman–Woodin hypothesis: obtain global failure of $\mathsf{GCH}$ from a hypothesis in the strong-cardinal/hypermeasurable region, or prove a lower bound above $o(\kappa)=\kappa^{++}$-type hypotheses.
- Build Radin-type forcings that add a club *and* force the tree property at $\kappa^+$, by replacing the square-like scale structure with a Neeman-style approach.
- Develop Radin analogues at *accessible* cardinals (e.g. adding generic clubs to $\aleph_\omega$-like objects) through Levy-collapse interleaving.

## 10. Worked Example / Concrete Special Case

**Degenerate case $\ell(u)=1$: Radin $=$ Prikry.** Let $\kappa$ be measurable, $j:V\to M=\mathrm{Ult}(V,U)$ with $U$ normal on $\kappa$. The recursion gives $u=\langle\kappa,U_0\rangle$ with $U_0 = \{X : \kappa \in j(X)\} = U$; it stops at length $1$ because $u\restriction 1 = \langle \kappa\rangle$ is in $M$ but $u\restriction 2$ would require $U\in M$, and $U \notin \mathrm{Ult}(V,U)$.

Conditions reduce to $\langle \alpha_0,\dots,\alpha_{n-1}, A\rangle$ with $\alpha_i<\kappa$ and $A\in U$: exactly Prikry forcing. The Prikry property is the classical argument: given $\sigma$ and $A\in U$, for each finite $s\in[\kappa]^{<\omega}$ colour $A$ by "$s$ forces $\sigma$ / forces $\neg\sigma$ / undecided"; $\kappa$-completeness gives a homogeneous $A_s \in U$, and the diagonal intersection $A^* = \triangle_s A_s$ works. Hence $\langle \, \rangle^\frown A^*$ decides $\sigma$ by a direct extension. The generic club is $C_u=\{\alpha_0<\alpha_1<\cdots\}$, of order type $\omega$: $\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)^{V[G]}=\omega$, and $\kappa$ is *singularized* — the preservation phenomenon is absent.

**First genuinely Radin case $\ell(u)=2$.** Now assume $o(\kappa)\ge 2$ and take $u=\langle\kappa,U_0,U_1\rangle$ where $U_1$ concentrates on the set $S=\{v : \ell(v)=1\}$ of measure sequences of length $1$ — i.e. on pairs $\langle\lambda,W\rangle$ with $W$ a normal measure on $\lambda<\kappa$. A generic club $C_u$ now contains two kinds of points: $U_0$-points (bare ordinals) and $U_1$-points (former measurables $\lambda$, each of which itself carries an induced Radin/Prikry generic club of order type $\omega$ below it). Counting: between consecutive $U_1$-points sit $\omega$ many $U_0$-points, so
$$\operatorname{ot}(C_u) = \omega\cdot\omega = \omega^2, \qquad \operatorname{cf}(\kappa)^{V[G]} = \omega .$$
So $\ell(u)=2$ still singularizes $\kappa$: the order type grew, but its cofinality did not.

**Where preservation starts.** Take $\ell(u)=\omega_1$ (requiring $o(\kappa)\ge\omega_1$). Then $\operatorname{ot}(C_u)$ has uncountable cofinality, no countable subset of $C_u$ is cofinal, and Radin's theorem gives $\kappa$ regular — in fact inaccessible — in $V[G]$, with every element of $C_u$ inaccessible. Pushing to $u$ derived from a $j$ with $V_{\kappa+2}\subseteq M$ makes $\kappa$ measurable again in $V[G]$.

This three-step ladder ($\ell=1 \to 2 \to \omega_1 \to$ hypermeasurable) is exactly what the generalization problem asks to reproduce with $P_\kappa\lambda$-measures in place of measures on $\kappa$: the $\ell=1$ analogue is supercompact Prikry forcing (known), the $\ell=2$ analogue is already delicate, and no uniform $\ell=\omega_1$ analogue with a verified Prikry property and full preservation is currently in the literature.
