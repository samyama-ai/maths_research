# Chow Ring Conjecture for Hyperkähler Manifolds (Beauville–Voisin)

> **Topic:** Algebraic & Differential Geometry · **ID:** `03-geometry/beauville-voisin-conjecture` · **Status:** partially-solved

## 1. Problem Statement / Conjecture

Let $X$ be a projective irreducible holomorphic symplectic (hyperkähler) manifold over $\mathbb{C}$: simply connected, compact, with $H^0(X,\Omega_X^2)=\mathbb{C}\sigma$ for a nowhere-degenerate $2$-form $\sigma$. Let $CH^*(X)_{\mathbb{Q}}$ be the Chow ring with rational coefficients and
$$cl:\ CH^*(X)_{\mathbb{Q}} \longrightarrow H^{2*}(X,\mathbb{Q})$$
the cycle class map. Write $DCH^*(X) \subseteq CH^*(X)_{\mathbb{Q}}$ for the $\mathbb{Q}$-subalgebra generated by $CH^1(X)_{\mathbb{Q}}$ (divisor classes).

**Conjecture (Beauville, 2007 — "weak splitting property").** The restriction $cl|_{DCH^*(X)}$ is injective. Equivalently: a polynomial in divisor classes that is homologically trivial is already zero in $CH^*(X)_{\mathbb{Q}}$.

**Conjecture (Voisin, 2008 — strong form).** The same injectivity holds for the subalgebra $DCH^*_c(X)$ generated by $CH^1(X)_{\mathbb{Q}}$ **together with** the Chern classes $c_i(T_X)\in CH^i(X)_{\mathbb{Q}}$.

$CH^*(X)_{\mathbb{Q}}$ is enormous (Mumford: $CH_0$ of a surface with $p_g>0$ is infinite-dimensional), so the content is that a small, geometrically defined subring is *cohomologically faithful*. A proof must produce, for each homologically trivial polynomial relation among divisors and Chern classes, a rational equivalence; a disproof must exhibit one hyperkähler $X$ and one such nonzero cycle in $\ker(cl)$.

## 2. Mathematical Foundations

**Chow groups.** $CH^k(X)=Z^k(X)/{\sim_{\mathrm{rat}}}$, graded by codimension under intersection product. For $X$ of dimension $2n$, $CH^{2n}(X)=CH_0(X)$.

**Bloch–Beilinson filtration.** Conjecturally there is a functorial, multiplicative filtration $F^\bullet$ on $CH^*(X)_{\mathbb{Q}}$ with
$$F^0CH^k = CH^k,\quad F^1CH^k = CH^k_{\hom},\quad F^i\cdot F^j\subseteq F^{i+j},\quad F^{k+1}CH^k=0,$$
and $\mathrm{Gr}_F^i CH^k$ controlled by $H^{2k-i}(X)$. Beauville's insight: for abelian varieties the Fourier–Mukai transform *splits* $F^\bullet$ canonically (Beauville decomposition $CH^k(A)_{\mathbb{Q}}=\bigoplus_s CH^k_s(A)$, where $[m]^*x=m^{2k-s}x$). The conjecture asserts a partial analogue for hyperkähler $X$: divisors and Chern classes lie in a canonical splitting piece $CH^*(X)_{(0)}$ on which $cl$ is injective.

**The Beauville–Voisin class.** For a projective K3 surface $S$ there is a canonical $c_S\in CH_0(S)$, the class of any point lying on a rational curve (rational curves exist by Bogomolov–Mumford), and
$$\mathrm{Im}\big(CH^1(S)\otimes CH^1(S)\to CH^2(S)\big)=\mathbb{Z}\,c_S,\qquad c_2(T_S)=24\,c_S .$$
For general hyperkähler $X^{2n}$ one expects a canonical $\mathfrak{o}_X\in CH_0(X)_{\mathbb{Q}}$ of degree $1$, represented by any point of a constant-cycle Lagrangian subvariety.

**Multiplicative Chow–Künneth (MCK) decomposition.** A self-dual decomposition $\Delta_X=\sum_{i=0}^{4n}\pi_i$ in $CH^{2n}(X\times X)_{\mathbb{Q}}$ with $\pi_i$ acting as projection onto $H^i(X)$, such that the small diagonal $\Delta^{sm}_X\subset X^3$ satisfies
$$\pi_k\circ \Delta^{sm}_X\circ(\pi_i\otimes\pi_j)=0 \quad\text{in } CH^{4n}(X^3)_{\mathbb{Q}} \text{ whenever } k\neq i+j .$$
An MCK decomposition makes $CH^*(X)_{\mathbb{Q}}=\bigoplus_{i,s}CH^i(X)_{(s)}$ a bigraded ring with $CH^i_{(s)}\cdot CH^j_{(t)}\subseteq CH^{i+j}_{(s+t)}$, and $CH^*_{(0)}\hookrightarrow H^*$ conjecturally. If divisors and $c_i(T_X)$ lie in $CH^*_{(0)}$, the Beauville–Voisin conjecture follows modulo Bloch–Beilinson.

**Voisin's $S_\bullet$ filtration.** $S_iCH_0(X)$ = classes supported on a subvariety of dimension $i$ swept by $i$-dimensional constant-cycle loci; conjecturally $S_iCH_0=F^{2n-i}$-complementary, giving $\dim\le n$ for the "constant" part — a Lagrangian bound.

## 3. History & State of the Art (SOTA)

- **1968.** Mumford proves $CH_0(S)$ is infinite-dimensional for $p_g(S)>0$, so K3 Chow groups are huge.
- **1983.** Bloch–Srinivas: decomposition of the diagonal links $CH_0$ to Hodge theory; the technical engine for all later work.
- **2004.** Beauville–Voisin, *On the Chow ring of a K3 surface*: existence of $c_S$, the two displayed relations above. The base case.
- **2007.** Beauville, *On the splitting of the Bloch–Beilinson filtration*: formulates the weak splitting property; verifies it for $S^{[2]}$ and $S^{[3]}$ ($S$ a K3) and for generalized Kummer $K_2(A)$.
- **2008.** Voisin extends the conjecture to Chern classes, proves it for $S^{[n]}$ in a range of $n$ growing linearly with the transcendental Betti number $b_{2,\mathrm{tr}}(S)$, and formulates the coisotropic/constant-cycle picture.
- **2013–2016.** Charles–Markman prove the standard conjectures for $K3^{[n]}$-type; Shen–Vial construct a Fourier decomposition and MCK for the Fano variety of lines on a cubic fourfold; Fu settles generalized Kummers.
- **2019–2021.** Fu–Laterveer–Vial connect the conjecture to the *generalized Franchetta conjecture* over moduli, and establish MCK for varieties of cohomological K3 type, including O'Grady-6.

State of the art: proven for the two classical deformation families in the known ranges, plus several sporadic families; open for a general hyperkähler manifold, and open in the strong (Chern class) form beyond low dimension.

## 4. Partial Results / Verified Cases

| Class | Result | Source |
|---|---|---|
| K3 surfaces ($\dim 2$) | Full: $D\cdot D'\in\mathbb{Z}c_S$, $c_2=24c_S$ | Beauville–Voisin 2004 |
| $S^{[2]}$, $S^{[3]}$ | Weak splitting | Beauville 2007 |
| $S^{[n]}$, $n$ bounded linearly in $b_{2,\mathrm{tr}}(S)$ | Weak splitting, plus Chern classes in low degree | Voisin 2008 |
| Generalized Kummer $K_n(A)$ | Weak splitting (Beauville decomposition on $A$ transported) | Fu 2015 (IMRN) |
| Birational models | Weak splitting is a birational invariant of irreducible symplectic varieties | Riess 2014 |
| $F(Y)$, Fano variety of lines on a cubic fourfold | MCK / Fourier decomposition; $c_i$ and divisors in $CH^*_{(0)}$ | Shen–Vial 2016 |
| Hilbert schemes $S^{[n]}$, all $n$ | Motivic (multiplicative) decomposition of $\Delta$ | Neguț–Oberdieck–Yin 2021 |
| Powers $S^n$ of a K3 | Tautological ring of $S^n$ injects into cohomology | Yin 2015 |
| O'Grady-6 (and Kummer-type) | MCK decompositions; Franchetta-type consequences | Fu–Laterveer–Vial 2019–2021 |

Concretely: for $X=S^{[n]}$ with $S$ a *general* K3 ($b_{2,\mathrm{tr}}=21$), Voisin's theorem covers all $n$ up to a bound in the forties; for $S$ with large Picard number the range shrinks.

## 5. Principal Obstacles

- **No Fourier transform.** For abelian varieties the Poincaré bundle gives $\mathcal{F}=p_{2*}(p_1^*(-)\cdot e^{\ell})$ and multiplication-by-$m$ maps that diagonalize $CH^*$. Hyperkähler manifolds have no group law and no universal correspondence; Shen–Vial build a substitute from the *incidence* correspondence on $F(Y)$, but that construction is case-specific and does not deform.
- **Bloch–Beilinson is itself conjectural.** Every conceptual proof route ("divisors lie in $F^0$-complement") presupposes a filtration whose existence is open; unconditional proofs must instead exhibit explicit rational equivalences.
- **Rational-curve arguments do not lift.** The K3 proof uses that any two points on a rational curve are rationally equivalent. In dimension $2n\ge 4$ the constant-cycle loci that should replace rational curves are conjecturally Lagrangian of dimension $n$, and their existence in the required abundance is unknown.
- **Deformation invariance fails.** Chow groups are not deformation invariants and not topological; results proved for $S^{[n]}$ do not transfer to a general deformation of $K3^{[n]}$-type, whose Picard group may be small but whose Chow ring is uncontrolled.
- **Combinatorial explosion.** Voisin's method verifies relations degree by degree using Nakajima operators and the $S^{[n]}$ cohomology model; the number of relations grows superpolynomially, which is exactly where the bound on $n$ comes from.
- **Only two deformation families are well understood.** $K3^{[n]}$, $\mathrm{Kum}_n$, OG6, OG10 exhaust the known examples; there is no structural handle on a hypothetical new family.

## 6. The Gap

The proven statements are: (i) full injectivity in dimension $2$; (ii) injectivity for $DCH^*$ on specific families and bounded parameters; (iii) MCK decompositions that *imply* injectivity **conditionally on Bloch–Beilinson**. The general statement asks for injectivity on every projective hyperkähler manifold with no bound on dimension, no assumption on the deformation type, and with Chern classes included.

Three concrete steps close the gap:

1. Construct a canonical $\mathfrak{o}_X\in CH_0(X)_{\mathbb{Q}}$ for arbitrary $X$ — equivalently, produce a constant-cycle Lagrangian subvariety on every projective hyperkähler manifold. This is open even for a general deformation of $K3^{[2]}$-type.
2. Prove that an MCK decomposition exists for all hyperkähler $X$ and that $CH^1(X)\subseteq CH^1_{(0)}$, $c_i(T_X)\subseteq CH^i_{(0)}$.
3. Prove $CH^*_{(0)}\hookrightarrow H^*$ unconditionally, i.e. remove the Bloch–Beilinson input — currently only possible in low dimension or when $CH_0$ is controlled by a surface.

## 7. Current Research (as of June 2026)

- **Motivic/tautological approach.** Neguț–Oberdieck–Yin's multiplicative motivic decomposition for $S^{[n]}$ is being pushed toward general moduli of sheaves on K3s; the aim is a uniform proof for all $K3^{[n]}$-type varieties with no bound on $n$. *(frontier — verify)*
- **Franchetta programme.** Fu–Laterveer–Vial's generalized Franchetta conjecture — that a cycle on the universal family restricting to a homologically trivial class on each fibre is fibrewise zero — is now the main organizing statement; new cases for OG10 and Debarre–Voisin twentyfolds are appearing. *(frontier — verify)*
- **Constant-cycle geometry.** Following Huybrechts and Voisin, work on constant-cycle Lagrangians, uniruled divisors and O'Grady's filtration on $CH_0$ continues (Paris–Orsay/Collège de France, Bonn, Cambridge, Milan, Strasbourg).
- **Hyperkähler moduli input.** Verbitsky's global Torelli and Markman's monodromy results are used to spread relations across a deformation family — the recurring difficulty is that Chow-theoretic statements are not open or closed conditions in moduli.

## 8. Future Work

- Prove existence of algebraically coisotropic subvarieties in every hyperkähler $X$ (Voisin's programme), which would supply $\mathfrak{o}_X$ and the $S_\bullet$ filtration simultaneously.
- Establish MCK deformation invariance within a locally trivial family, or prove that the weak splitting property is a deformation-invariant condition — either would immediately globalize the $S^{[n]}$ results.
- Extend Yin's tautological-ring machinery from $S^n$ to $X^n$ for hyperkähler $X$, giving a presentation by generators and relations in which injectivity is checkable.
- Test the strong form numerically: search for a homologically trivial polynomial in $c_i(T_X)$ and divisors on OG10 ($\dim=10$, $b_2=24$) that current methods cannot kill — a candidate counterexample would be as informative as a proof.

## 10. Worked Example / Concrete Special Case

**The quartic K3 surface $S=\{x_0^4+x_1^4+x_2^4+x_3^4=0\}\subset\mathbb{P}^3$ (Fermat quartic).**

*Step 1 — the canonical class.* $S$ contains $48$ lines, e.g. $L=\{x_0+\zeta x_1=0,\ x_2+\eta x_3=0\}$ with $\zeta^4=\eta^4=-1$. Any two points $p,q\in L\cong\mathbb{P}^1$ satisfy $p-q=\mathrm{div}(f)$ for a rational function $f$ on $L$, hence $[p]=[q]$ in $CH_0(S)$. Set $c_S:=[p]$. Beauville–Voisin show this class is independent of which rational curve is used: any two rational curves on a K3 meet after moving in their linear systems, and the intersection point forces the classes to agree.

*Step 2 — divisor products.* Let $h=\mathcal{O}_S(1)$, so $\deg h^2=4$. The claim $h^2=4c_S$ in $CH_0(S)$: take a plane $H\subset\mathbb{P}^3$ containing $L$. Then $H\cap S=L+C$ with $C$ a residual plane cubic, and $h^2 = h\cdot(L+C)$. Now $h\cdot L$ is a degree-$1$ zero-cycle supported on $L$, so $h\cdot L=c_S$. The cubic $C$ meets $L$ in $L\cdot C = 3 - L^2 \cdot(\dots)$ points; running the same argument with the pencil of planes through $L$ and using $L^2=-2$ on a K3 (adjunction: $L^2=2p_a(L)-2=-2$) gives $h\cdot C=3c_S$. Hence
$$h^2 = c_S+3c_S = 4c_S,$$
matching the degree. So the homologically trivial class $h^2-4c_S$ is zero in $CH_0$ — an instance of Section 1.

*Step 3 — Chern classes.* $c_1(T_S)=0$ and $\int_S c_2(T_S)=\chi_{\mathrm{top}}(S)=24$. The Beauville–Voisin theorem upgrades this numerical statement to $c_2(T_S)=24\,c_S$ in $CH_0(S)_{\mathbb{Q}}$ — a strictly stronger assertion, since $CH_0(S)$ is infinite-dimensional and $\ker(\deg)$ is huge.

*Step 4 — up one level.* On $X=S^{[2]}$ ($\dim 4$, $b_2=23$), $CH^1(X)_{\mathbb{Q}}$ is spanned by $\mu(h)$ (the class induced from $h$) and $\delta=\tfrac12 E$, half the exceptional divisor of the Hilbert–Chow map. The weak splitting property here says, for example, that the degree-$4$ relation
$$\mu(h)^4 - 6\,\mu(h)^2\delta^2 + 3\,\delta^4 \;=\; \big(\textstyle\int_X \text{same}\big)\cdot \mathfrak{o}_X$$
holds in $CH_0(X)_{\mathbb{Q}}$ and not merely in $H^8(X,\mathbb{Q})\cong\mathbb{Q}$, with $\mathfrak{o}_X$ the class of a point on a constant-cycle Lagrangian surface (e.g. $\{p+q: p,q\in L\}\cong\mathbb{P}^2$ for $L$ one of the $48$ lines). Beauville verified all such relations for $S^{[2]}$; whether the analogue holds for every hyperkähler manifold is the open problem.
