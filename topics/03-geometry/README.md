# Algebraic & Differential Geometry

Algebraic varieties, manifolds, symplectic geometry, Riemannian manifolds, curvature, discrete & convex geometry

## Problems Index

* 🟢 [Abhyankar Conjecture](./abhyankar-conjecture.md) — Classifies finite groups appearing as Galois groups of affine curves.
* 🔴 [Abundance Conjecture](./abundance-conjecture.md) — The canonical bundle of a minimal projective variety is semiample.
* 🟢 [Alexandrov's Soap Bubble Theorem](./alexandrov-soap-bubble-theorem.md) — Proves embedded surfaces with constant mean curvature in Euclidean space are spheres.
* 🟢 [Andre-Oort Conjecture](./andre-oort-conjecture.md) — Subvarieties containing dense sets of special points in Shimura varieties are special.
* 🔴 [Arakelov's Adjunction Formula Conjecture](./arakelov-adjunction-conjecture.md) — Generalizes classical intersection adjunction formulas to heights in arithmetic Arakelov geometry.
* 🟡 [Arnold Conjecture](./arnold-conjecture.md) — Fixed points of Hamiltonian diffeomorphisms are bounded by Betti numbers.
* 🔴 [Atiyah-Floer Conjecture](./atiyah-floer-conjecture.md) — Instanton Floer homology is isomorphic to Lagrangian intersection Floer homology.
* 🟡 [Ax-Schanuel Conjecture](./ax-schanuel-conjecture.md) — A functional transcendence statement bounding intersections of algebraic and exponential varieties.
* 🟢 [Bando-Mabuchi-Mukai Conjecture](./bando-mabuchi-mukai-conjecture.md) — Kahler-Einstein metrics exist on Fano manifolds with reductive automorphism groups.
* 🔴 [Bass-Quillen Conjecture](./bass-quillen-conjecture.md) — Finitely generated projective modules over polynomial rings of regular rings are free.
* 🔴 [BCOV Conjecture](./bcov-conjecture.md) — Relates Gromov-Witten invariants of Calabi-Yau manifolds to analytic torsions.
* 🔴 [Beilinson Conjectures](./beilinson-conjectures.md) — Relates special values of L-functions to regulators in algebraic K-theory.
* 🔴 [Berger Conjecture](./berger-conjecture.md) — Riemannian manifolds with reduced holonomy are locally symmetric or locally irreducible.
* 🔴 [Betti Langlands Conjecture](./betti-langlands-conjecture.md) — A topological version of the Langlands correspondence for character varieties.
* 🔴 [Bloch's Conjecture on Zero Cycles](./bloch-zero-cycles-conjecture.md) — Suggests the vanishing of geometric genus implies a trivial Chow group.
* 🔴 [Bombieri-Lang Conjecture](./bombieri-lang-conjecture.md) — Rational points on general type algebraic varieties are not Zariski dense.
* 🟢 [Borisov-Alexeev-Borisov Conjecture](./borisov-alexeev-borisov-conjecture.md) — Fano varieties with epsilon kawamata log terminal singularities form bounded families.
* 🔴 [Bott Conjecture](./bott-conjecture.md) — Posits that simply connected manifolds with positive sectional curvature are rationally elliptic.
* 🔴 [Bounded Negativity Conjecture](./bounded-negativity-conjecture.md) — The self-intersection of irreducible curves on algebraic surfaces is bounded below.
* 🟢 [Busemann-Petty Problem](./busemann-petty-problem.md) — Do symmetric convex bodies with larger hyperplane sections have larger volume?
* 🔴 [Campana Conjecture](./campana-conjecture.md) — Special varieties are exactly the varieties with potentially dense rational points.
* 🔴 [Caratheodory Conjecture](./caratheodory-conjecture.md) — Every closed convex surface has at least two umbilic points.
* 🔴 [Cartan-Hadamard Conjecture](./cartan-hadamard-conjecture.md) — The isoperimetric inequality holds in simply connected spaces with non-positive curvature.
* 🟢 [Generalized Smale Conjecture](./generalized-smale-conjecture.md) — Diffeomorphism groups of spherical space forms retract to their isometry groups.
* 🔴 [Geometric Langlands Conjecture](./geometric-langlands-conjecture.md) — Equates D-modules on the moduli space of bundles to Galois representations.
* 🔴 [Gieseker Conjecture](./gieseker-conjecture.md) — Classifies stable vector bundles on algebraic surfaces using characteristic classes.
* 🟢 [Goresky-MacPherson Conjecture](./goresky-macpherson-conjecture.md) — Intersection homology determines topological invariance for certain classes of singular spaces.
* 🟡 [Green Conjecture](./green-conjecture.md) — Relates the Clifford index of a curve to its canonical syzygies.
* 🔴 [Green-Griffiths Conjecture](./green-griffiths-conjecture.md) — Entire holomorphic curves in general type varieties are degenerate.
* 🔴 [Griffiths Conjecture](./griffiths-conjecture.md) — Positive vector bundles on Kahler manifolds admit metrics with positive curvature.
* 🔴 [Gromov Conjecture](./gromov-conjecture.md) — Relates the scalar curvature of a manifold to its simplicial volume.
* 🔴 [Grothendieck P-Curvature Conjecture](./grothendieck-p-curvature-conjecture.md) — Linear differential equations have algebraic solutions if their p-curvatures vanish.
* 🟡 [Grothendieck-Serre Conjecture](./grothendieck-serre-conjecture.md) — Principal bundles for reductive algebraic groups are trivial over regular local rings.
* 🟢 [Gudkov Conjecture](./gudkov-conjecture.md) — Relates the arrangement of ovals of real algebraic curves to congruences.
* 🔴 [Halperin-Carlsson Conjecture](./halperin-carlsson-conjecture.md) — Bounds the sum of Betti numbers for spaces admitting free torus actions.
* 🟢 [Hamilton-Tian Conjecture](./hamilton-tian-conjecture.md) — Kahler-Ricci flow on Fano manifolds converges to Kahler-Ricci solitons.
* 🔴 [Hodge Conjecture](./hodge-conjecture.md)
* 🔴 [Hofer Conjecture](./hofer-conjecture.md) — The Hofer metric on the group of Hamiltonian diffeomorphisms is strictly intrinsic.
* 🟡 [Homological Mirror Symmetry](./homological-mirror-symmetry.md) — Derived categories of coherent sheaves are equivalent to Fukaya categories.
* 🔴 [Hopf Conjecture](./hopf-conjecture.md) — Compact Riemannian manifolds with positive sectional curvature have positive Euler characteristic.
* 🟢 [Hsiang-Lawson Conjecture](./hsiang-lawson-conjecture.md) — The Clifford torus minimizes area among equatorial tori in the 3-sphere.
* 🟡 [Iitaka Conjecture](./iitaka-conjecture.md) — The Kodaira dimension of a fiber space is superadditive over its base.
* 🟢 [Katz-Klemm-Vafa Conjecture](./katz-klemm-vafa-conjecture.md) — Gromov-Witten invariants of K3 surfaces can be calculated using modular forms.
* 🔴 [Kobayashi Conjecture](./kobayashi-conjecture.md) — A generic hypersurface of high degree in projective space is Kobayashi hyperbolic.
* 🔴 [Kollar's Conjecture on Local Fundamental Groups](./kollar-local-fundamental-group-conjecture.md) — Questions the finiteness properties of local algebraic fundamental groups of singularity links.
* 🔴 [Kuga Conjecture](./kuga-conjecture.md) — Certain Hodge cycles on abelian varieties are absolute Hodge classes.
* 🔴 [L-space Conjecture](./l-space-conjecture.md) — Relates Heegaard Floer homology to the existence of orderable fundamental groups.
* 🔴 [Lang Conjecture](./lang-conjecture.md) — Hyperbolic complex projective varieties lack rational and elliptic subvarieties.
* 🟢 [Lawson Conjecture](./lawson-conjecture.md) — The Clifford torus is the only minimally embedded torus in the 3-sphere.
* 🔴 [Lelong's Conjecture](./lelong-conjecture.md) — States that positive closed currents with regular constant densities are integration currents.
* 🟢 [Lichnerowicz Conjecture](./lichnerowicz-conjecture.md) — Harmonic manifolds are locally symmetric spaces or flat manifolds.
