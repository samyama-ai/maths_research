# Large Deviations for Schramm-Loewner Evolution

> **Topic:** Probability & Stochastic Processes · **ID:** `09-probability/large-deviations-schramm-loewner-evolution` · **Status:** partially-solved

## 1. Problem Statement / Conjecture

Schramm–Loewner evolution $\mathrm{SLE}_\kappa$ is a one-parameter family of random fractal curves. The large-deviation programme asks for the exact exponential cost of atypical behaviour in the two degenerate limits $\kappa\to 0^+$ (curves become deterministic geodesics) and $\kappa\to\infty$ (hulls become space-filling), and for a rate function with intrinsic geometric meaning.

**Established core (chordal, $\kappa\to0^+$).** The laws of chordal $\mathrm{SLE}_\kappa$ in $(\mathbb{H},0,\infty)$ satisfy a large deviation principle (LDP) with speed $1/\kappa$ and good rate function the *Loewner energy* $I^{\mathbb H}(\gamma;0,\infty)$.

**Open components.** A complete theory requires, and does not yet have:

1. **(LD-∞ chordal/whole-plane)** An LDP for $\mathrm{SLE}_\kappa$ as $\kappa\to\infty$ outside the radial case; only radial $\mathrm{SLE}_\infty$ is proved.
2. **(LD-loop)** An LDP for Zhan's $\mathrm{SLE}_\kappa$ *loop measure* (an infinite measure) as $\kappa\to0^+$, with rate function the loop Loewner energy $I^L$, in a topology strong enough to encode the curve, not merely its driving data.
3. **(LD-CLE)** A large-deviation description of conformal loop ensembles $\mathrm{CLE}_\kappa$ as $\kappa\to 8/3^+$ (equivalently, central charge $c\to 0^-$), conjecturally with rate function $\sum_j I^L(\gamma_j)$ over the loops.
4. **(LD-fixed-$\kappa$)** Large deviations for fixed $\kappa\in(0,8)$: sharp exponential asymptotics for events such as the trace being $\varepsilon$-close to a prescribed curve, or extreme multifractal/winding behaviour, where no small parameter linearises the problem.

A resolution of any item means: a rate function that is *good* (compact sublevel sets), matching upper and lower bounds in a specified topology, and an identification of the rate function in conformally invariant terms.

## 2. Mathematical Foundations

**Chordal Loewner chain.** For a continuous driving function $W:[0,T]\to\mathbb{R}$, solve for each $z\in\mathbb{H}$
$$\partial_t g_t(z)=\frac{2}{g_t(z)-W_t},\qquad g_0(z)=z .$$
Let $\tau(z)$ be the blow-up time and $K_t=\{z:\tau(z)\le t\}$; $g_t:\mathbb{H}\setminus K_t\to\mathbb{H}$ is conformal with $g_t(z)=z+2t/z+O(z^{-2})$. Chordal $\mathrm{SLE}_\kappa$ is the chain driven by $W_t=\sqrt{\kappa}B_t$; it is generated by a continuous trace $\gamma$ (Rohde–Schramm 2005; Lawler–Viklund for $\kappa=8$), simple for $\kappa\le4$, self-touching for $4<\kappa<8$, space-filling for $\kappa\ge8$.

**Dirichlet energy of the driver.** For $W$ absolutely continuous,
$$I_T(W)=\frac{1}{2}\int_0^T \dot W_t^2\,dt,\qquad I_T(W)=+\infty \text{ otherwise.}$$
By Schilder's theorem, $(\sqrt\kappa B)_{\kappa>0}$ satisfies an LDP on $C_0([0,T],\mathbb{R})$ with sup-norm topology, speed $1/\kappa$, rate $I_T$.

**Loewner energy.** For a chordal curve $\gamma$ in $(\mathbb{H},0,\infty)$,
$$I^{\mathbb H}(\gamma;0,\infty)=I_\infty(W)$$
where $W$ is the driving function of $\gamma$ in capacity parametrisation. It is conformally invariant: $I^D(\gamma;a,b)$ is well defined for any simply connected $D$ with boundary points $a,b$, and is additive under concatenation with a reversibility property. For a Jordan curve $\gamma\subset\hat{\mathbb C}$ the *loop Loewner energy* $I^L(\gamma)$ satisfies $I^L(\gamma)=0$ iff $\gamma$ is a circle, and $I^L(\gamma)<\infty$ iff $\gamma$ is a Weil–Petersson quasicircle.

**Identifications (Wang, Invent. Math. 2019).** $I^L$ equals $1/\pi$ times the Takhtajan–Teo universal Liouville action on the Weil–Petersson universal Teichmüller space $T_0(1)$, and admits an expression through $\zeta$-regularised determinants of Laplacians; equivalently, if $f,h$ uniformise the two complementary components,
$$I^L(\gamma)=\frac{1}{\pi}\left(\int_{\mathbb{D}}\big|\nabla\log|f'|\big|^2 + \int_{\mathbb{D}^*}\big|\nabla\log|h'|\big|^2\right)+4\log\big|f'(0)/h'(\infty)\big| .$$

**Radial / measure-driven chains.** The Loewner–Kufarev equation for a family of probability measures $\rho_t$ on $S^1$,
$$\partial_t g_t(z)=g_t(z)\int_{S^1}\frac{e^{i\theta}+g_t(z)}{e^{i\theta}-g_t(z)}\,\rho_t(d\theta),$$
generalises radial Loewner evolution (point masses). For $\rho_t(d\theta)=\varrho(t,\theta)\,d\theta$ the Loewner–Kufarev energy is the Donsker–Varadhan-type functional
$$S(\rho)=\frac{1}{2}\int_0^\infty\!\!\int_{S^1}\big(\partial_\theta\sqrt{\varrho(t,\theta)}\big)^2 d\theta\,dt ,$$
(up to normalisation of $d\theta$), $+\infty$ if $\rho_t$ is not a.c. with $\sqrt{\varrho}\in H^1$.

## 3. History & State of the Art (SOTA)

- **2000.** Schramm introduces SLE via the Loewner equation and conformal Markov property.
- **2005.** Rohde–Schramm establish trace continuity, the phase transition at $\kappa=4,8$, and the failure of naive continuity of the Loewner transform $W\mapsto\gamma$.
- **2017.** Friz–Shekhar prove existence and Hölder regularity of the trace for finite-energy (Cameron–Martin) drivers and for non-constant $\kappa$ — the technical key to any contraction principle.
- **2019.** Wang proves the chordal $\mathrm{SLE}_{0+}$ LDP with rate function the Loewner energy, and (Invent. Math.) identifies the loop energy with the universal Liouville action and with Weil–Petersson quasicircles, linking probability to Teichmüller theory and to $c\to -\infty$ Liouville CFT.
- **2020.** Viklund–Wang relate Loewner and Dirichlet energies through conformal welding and GFF flow lines. Ang–Park–Wang prove the $\kappa\to\infty$ LDP for **radial** SLE with rate function $S$.
- **2021.** Rohde–Wang: sharp regularity of finite-energy driving functions and loop energy.
- **2022.** Wang's *Probability Surveys* survey consolidates the field; Ang–Park–Pfeffer–Sheffield connect Brownian loop soups and central charge, feeding the CLE side.
- **2024.** Peltola–Wang (JEMS) solve the **multichordal** $\kappa\to0^+$ case: the rate function is a sum of Loewner energies, minimisers are described by real rational functions, and the partition-function asymptotics involve $\zeta$-determinants of Laplacians.
- **2020s.** Bishop's characterisation of Weil–Petersson curves via $\beta$-numbers and minimal surfaces gives independent geometric handles on the finite-energy class.

## 4. Partial Results / Verified Cases

| Regime | Status | Rate function / speed |
|---|---|---|
| Chordal $\mathrm{SLE}_{0+}$ in $(\mathbb{H},0,\infty)$ | **Proved** (Wang 2019) | $I^{\mathbb H}(\gamma)$, speed $1/\kappa$ |
| Driving-function level, any $\kappa\to0$ | **Proved** (Schilder) | $\frac12\int\dot W^2$, speed $1/\kappa$ |
| Finite-energy drivers ⇒ trace exists, is a quasi-arc | **Proved** (Friz–Shekhar 2017; Rohde–Wang 2021) | — |
| Multichordal $N$-curve $\kappa\to0^+$, fixed boundary data | **Proved** (Peltola–Wang 2024) | $\sum_{j=1}^N I^{D}(\gamma_j)$; minimisers ↔ real rational functions of degree $N+1$ |
| Whole-plane / loop energy: $I^L<\infty \iff$ Weil–Petersson quasicircle | **Proved** (Wang 2019; Takhtajan–Teo 2006) | — |
| **Radial** $\mathrm{SLE}_\kappa$, $\kappa\to\infty$ | **Proved** (Ang–Park–Wang 2020) | Loewner–Kufarev energy $S(\rho)$, speed $\kappa$ |
| Chordal / whole-plane $\kappa\to\infty$ | **Open** | conjecturally a Loewner–Kufarev-type $S$ |
| $\mathrm{SLE}_\kappa$ loop measure $\kappa\to0^+$ | **Open** (normalisation of an infinite measure) | conjecturally $I^L$ |
| $\mathrm{CLE}_\kappa$, $\kappa\to 8/3^+$ | **Open / conjectural** | conjecturally $\sum_j I^L(\gamma_j)$ |
| Fixed $\kappa\in(0,8)$, small-ball / extreme events | **Open** (only crude bounds) | no rate function known |

Parameter ranges matter: all proved $\kappa\to0$ statements live in the simple phase $\kappa\le4$ in the limit; the multichordal results require $\kappa\to0$ with the number of curves fixed and $N$-independence of the topology of the link pattern.

## 5. Principal Obstacles

- **Discontinuity of the Loewner transform.** The contraction principle needs $W\mapsto\gamma$ continuous. In sup-norm it is *not*: drivers of Hölder-$1/2$ norm above $4$ can generate non-curves (Kufarev's example, Lind's bounds). Continuity is only recovered on finite-energy sets, so the transfer is a delicate energy-level-by-energy-level argument, and this is exactly what blocks stronger topologies (Hausdorff on the whole trace, or uniform in the parametrisation).
- **Non-compactness of the loop measure.** Zhan's SLE loop measure is $\sigma$-finite and infinite; there is no probability measure to run an LDP on, and no canonical normalisation that survives $\kappa\to0$. Standard Varadhan/Bryc machinery assumes probability measures.
- **No small parameter for fixed $\kappa$.** Away from $\kappa\to0,\infty$, the driver is a fixed-diffusivity Brownian motion. The natural rate would have to come from a nonlinear, non-Gaussian variational problem; Girsanov gives exact tilts only for drift perturbations, and the resulting curve-space functional has no known closed form.
- **Multiplicative chaos / GFF couplings resist exponential estimates.** Imaginary-geometry and quantum-zipper couplings are measure-preserving identities, not exponential-moment statements; exponential tightness in the coupled field is unproved.
- **$\kappa\to\infty$ is not a small perturbation of a curve.** The hull becomes space-filling; the correct state space is families of measures/hulls, not curves, and chordal capacity parametrisation degenerates, so the radial proof (Donsker–Varadhan for the occupation measure of fast Brownian motion on $S^1$) has no direct chordal analogue where the driver lives on the unbounded line and does not equilibrate.
- **CLE has no driving function.** There is no single Loewner chain encoding a full ensemble; the conjectural $\sum_j I^L(\gamma_j)$ has no proof strategy through Schilder.

## 6. The Gap

Proved: an LDP for one chordal curve at $\kappa\to0$, plus its multichordal extension, plus radial $\kappa\to\infty$. Wanted: the same for objects that are *not* a finite family of curves parametrised by capacity in a fixed domain.

The exact step: promote the finite-energy continuity of the Loewner transform from "continuity on compact sublevel sets of $I$ in sup-norm on drivers" to a statement that (a) survives an infinite ambient measure (loop case), (b) holds uniformly over a family of chains driven by measures rather than points (chordal $\kappa\to\infty$), and (c) yields exponential tightness for ensembles. Concretely, no proof currently produces exponential tightness of $\mathrm{SLE}_\kappa$ *traces* in the Hausdorff topology without first passing through the driver, and the loop and CLE problems have no driver to pass through.

## 7. Current Research (as of June 2026)

- **IHES / MIT / Fudan (Wang and collaborators).** Loewner energy as a Kähler-geometric object on $T_0(1)$; energy identities via $\zeta$-determinants; foliations of Weil–Petersson quasicircles by Loewner–Kufarev flows (Viklund–Wang). Active work on an LDP for the SLE loop measure via a normalised "rooted" loop that restores finiteness *(frontier — verify)*.
- **Helsinki / Aalto (Peltola and coauthors).** $\kappa\to0$ asymptotics of multiple-SLE partition functions, links to real enumerative geometry (Shapiro–Shapiro type counts of real rational functions) and to minimal-energy configurations in general domains.
- **KTH (Viklund, Johansson school).** Deterministic Loewner theory: modulus of continuity of the transform, energy of foliations, and the geometry of the finite-energy class.
- **Columbia / Cambridge / Chicago (Ang, Park, Pfeffer, Sheffield, Miller, Gwynne).** LQG and Brownian-loop-soup routes to central-charge asymptotics; the $c\to0^-$ CLE large-deviation picture is the stated target *(frontier — verify)*.
- **Rough-path community (Friz and coauthors).** Reconstructing SLE-type flows under rough drivers; Schilder-type theorems for non-constant $\kappa$ and for SLE$_\kappa(\rho)$.

## 8. Future Work

- Prove chordal and whole-plane $\mathrm{SLE}_\infty$ LDPs by replacing capacity time with a measure-valued state space and adapting Donsker–Varadhan estimates to a non-compact driver space.
- Define a normalised loop functional (e.g. conditioning on the loop meeting a fixed disk, then removing the conditioning) for which $I^L$ is the rate function; this is the cleanest route to a "loop LDP".
- Establish exponential tightness of SLE traces directly in the Hausdorff metric, bypassing the driver — likely through multi-scale conformal-radius estimates.
- Extend the multichordal result to $N\to\infty$ jointly with $\kappa\to0$, where the minimisers should converge to a foliation and $S(\rho)$ should appear as the limiting energy.
- Formulate and test the CLE conjecture numerically: sample $\mathrm{CLE}_\kappa$ for $\kappa\downarrow 8/3$ and check $-\,(\kappa-8/3)\log$-probabilities against $\sum_j I^L$.
- Fixed-$\kappa$ moderate deviations for the winding number and for the multifractal spectrum of the harmonic measure (Gwynne–Miller–Sun-type results give a.s. spectra; the deviation cost is unknown).

## 10. Worked Example / Concrete Special Case

**Linear driver: exact cost of a tilted SLE.** Fix $c\in\mathbb{R}$, $T>0$ and target driver $w_t=ct$, $t\in[0,T]$. Its Dirichlet energy is
$$I_T(w)=\frac12\int_0^T c^2\,dt=\frac{c^2T}{2}.$$

*Exact probability via Girsanov.* Let $W_t=\sqrt\kappa B_t$. On $\{ \sup_{t\le T}|W_t-ct|\le\varepsilon\}$, Cameron–Martin gives
$$\mathbb{P}\big[\textstyle\sup_{t\le T}|W_t-ct|\le\varepsilon\big]=\mathbb{E}\Big[\exp\Big(\frac{c}{\sqrt\kappa}B_T-\frac{c^2T}{2\kappa}\Big)\mathbf 1_{\{\sup_{t\le T}|W_t|\le\varepsilon\}}\Big].$$
Since $|B_T|\le \varepsilon/\sqrt{\kappa}$ on the event, the prefactor is $e^{\pm c\varepsilon/\kappa}$, so
$$\kappa\log\mathbb{P}\big[\textstyle\sup_{t\le T}|W_t-ct|\le\varepsilon\big]=-\frac{c^2T}{2}+O(\varepsilon)+\kappa\log\mathbb{P}\big[\textstyle\sup_{t\le T}|\sqrt\kappa B_t|\le\varepsilon\big].$$
The last term is $O(\kappa\log(1/\kappa))\to0$ for fixed $\varepsilon$. Hence
$$\lim_{\varepsilon\to0}\lim_{\kappa\to0}\ \kappa\log\mathbb{P}\big[\cdots\big]=-I_T(w)=-\frac{c^2T}{2}.$$

*Transfer to curves.* $w$ has finite energy, so by Friz–Shekhar the chain driven by $w$ generates a curve $\gamma^c$ (an analytic arc in $\mathbb{H}$, obtainable in closed form after the shift $\tilde g_t=g_t-ct$, which turns the Loewner ODE into the autonomous flow $\dot{\tilde g}=2/\tilde g-c$ with fixed points $\tilde g=\pm$roots of $c\,\tilde g^{\,2}=2\tilde g$-type algebra). By Wang's theorem $I^{\mathbb H}(\gamma^c)=c^2T/2$, so for the trace itself
$$\mathbb{P}\big[\mathrm{SLE}_\kappa\ \varepsilon\text{-close to }\gamma^c\big]=e^{-\frac{c^2T}{2\kappa}(1+o(1))}.$$

*Where it breaks.* Take instead $w_t=\lambda\sqrt{t}$. Then $\dot w_t=\lambda/(2\sqrt t)$ and
$$I_T(w)=\frac12\int_0^T\frac{\lambda^2}{4t}\,dt=+\infty,$$
yet the generated curve is a perfectly nice straight segment leaving $\mathbb{R}$ at angle $\theta(\lambda)\neq\pi/2$ for $\lambda \neq 0$, and for $|\lambda|>4$ it is not even a simple curve. So a geometrically tame curve can carry infinite Loewner energy, and the driver-to-curve map is discontinuous exactly at this threshold. This one-line example is the concrete face of the obstacle in Section 5: the LDP must be run on energy sublevel sets where the transform is continuous, and every open item in Section 1 is a setting where such sublevel sets are not available.
