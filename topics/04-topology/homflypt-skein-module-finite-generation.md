# HOMFLYPT Skein Module Finite Generation

> **Topic:** Topology & Knot Theory · **ID:** `04-topology/homflypt-skein-module-finite-generation` · **Status:** partially-solved

## 1. Problem Statement / Conjecture

Let $M$ be a closed, oriented $3$-manifold and let $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(M;R)$ denote its HOMFLYPT (Conway/Homfly) skein module over $R=\mathbb{Z}[v^{\pm1},z^{\pm1}]$.

**Conjecture (HOMFLYPT finiteness).** For every closed oriented $3$-manifold $M$,
$$\dim_{\mathbb{Q}(v,z)}\ \mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(M;\mathbb{Q}(v,z)) < \infty .$$

Two sharpenings are part of the problem:

- **(Integral form.)** Is $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(M;R)$ finitely generated as an $R$-module? This is expected to be **false** in general: the Kauffman bracket analogue already fails for $S^1\times S^2$ (Hoste–Przytycki), where infinitely many torsion summands appear. A complete answer must describe the torsion.
- **(Boundary form.)** For $M$ compact with $\partial M\neq\emptyset$, finiteness is false — handlebody skein modules are free of countably infinite rank — so the conjecture is a statement about *closed* manifolds, or about relative skein modules with a fixed finite boundary point set.

A complete proof must produce, for each closed $M$, a finite spanning set of $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(M;\mathbb{Q}(v,z))$ over the **two-variable** field $\mathbb{Q}(v,z)$; a disproof must exhibit a closed $M$ and an infinite $\mathbb{Q}(v,z)$-linearly independent family of skein classes.

## 2. Mathematical Foundations

Let $\mathcal{L}^{fr}(M)$ be the set of isotopy classes of framed oriented links in $M$, including the empty link $\emptyset$. Let $R\mathcal{L}^{fr}(M)$ be the free $R$-module on this set. Let $S_{\mathrm{H}}$ be the submodule generated by all expressions

$$v^{-1}L_{+}\;-\;v\,L_{-}\;-\;z\,L_{0},$$

where $(L_+,L_-,L_0)$ is a **skein triple**: three framed oriented links identical outside a ball $B\subset M$, and inside $B$ equal to a positive crossing, a negative crossing, and the oriented smoothing respectively; together with the **framing relation**

$$L^{(1)}\;-\;v^{-1}L ,$$

where $L^{(1)}$ is $L$ with the framing of one component increased by $+1$. Then

$$\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(M;R)\;=\;R\mathcal{L}^{fr}(M)\big/ S_{\mathrm{H}} .$$

A consequence of the two relations is the **value of a trivial component**: if $U$ bounds a disk disjoint from $L$,
$$L\sqcup U \;=\; \delta\, L,\qquad \delta=\frac{v^{-1}-v}{z}.$$

Base change $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(M;F)=\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(M;R)\otimes_R F$ for $F=\mathbb{Q}(v,z)$ kills torsion.

**Known structure theorems.**

- **(Turaev 1988.)** For the solid torus $V=S^1\times D^2$, $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(V;R)$ is a free $R$-module, isomorphic as an algebra to the polynomial ring
 $$\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(V)\cong R[x_i : i\in\mathbb{Z}\setminus\{0\}],$$
 with $x_i$ the class of the core curve wrapping $|i|$ times with sign $\mathrm{sgn}(i)$; equivalently, a basis is indexed by finite multisets of nontrivial conjugacy classes of $\pi_1(V)=\mathbb{Z}$.
- **(Przytycki 1992.)** For a handlebody $H_g$, $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(H_g;R)$ is free with basis the finite multisets of nontrivial conjugacy classes of the free group $F_g$ — countably infinite rank for $g\ge1$.
- **Surgery presentation.** If $M$ is obtained from $S^3$ by surgery on a framed link $L$, then $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(M)$ is the quotient of $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(S^3\setminus L)$ by the **handle-slide relations** $x - \mathrm{sl}_i(x)$ for slides over each surgery solid torus. Finiteness is therefore the assertion that finitely many slide relations collapse an infinite-rank module to a finite-dimensional one.
- **Classical limit.** At $v=q^{N}$, $z=q-q^{-1}$, $q\to 1$, HOMFLYPT skein theory degenerates to the coordinate ring of the $SL_N(\mathbb{C})$-character variety of $\pi_1(M)$ (Sikora); the $N=2$ case is Bullock's theorem for the Kauffman bracket. Finiteness of skein modules is the quantum shadow of $\dim X_{SL_N}(\pi_1 M)$ being finite with finitely many components.

## 3. History & State of the Art (SOTA)

- **1985.** The HOMFLYPT polynomial is introduced (Freyd–Yetter–Hoste–Lickorish–Millett–Ocneanu; independently Przytycki–Traczyk), giving the skein relation above for links in $S^3$.
- **1988–1991.** Turaev computes the Conway skein module of the solid torus; Przytycki formalises skein modules of arbitrary $3$-manifolds and poses the finiteness/structure questions. The handlebody case is settled in 1992.
- **1993–1995.** Hoste–Przytycki compute $(2,\infty)$- and Kauffman-bracket skein modules of lens spaces and of $S^1\times S^2$, discovering **infinite torsion** over $\mathbb{Z}[A^{\pm1}]$ — the first evidence that finiteness must be stated over a field of fractions.
- **2001.** Gilmer–Zhong compute the HOMFLYPT skein module of $S^1\times S^2$ and prove a connected-sum formula: over a suitable localisation, $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(M_1\\# M_2)\cong \mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(M_1)\otimes\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(M_2)$. Finiteness is thus closed under connected sum.
- **2017.** Morton–Samuelson identify the HOMFLYPT skein algebra of the torus $T^2\times I$ with a specialisation of the elliptic Hall algebra — an infinite-dimensional but completely understood object that controls skein modules of all torus-bundle and Dehn-filling constructions.
- **2019–2021 (breakthrough).** Gunningham–Jordan–Safronov prove the **Witten finiteness conjecture**: for a closed oriented $3$-manifold, the skein module built from $\mathrm{Rep}_q(G)$ at generic $q$ is finite-dimensional over $\mathbb{C}$. The proof is not diagrammatic: it realises the skein module as factorization homology, i.e. the value of a TQFT on $M$, and deduces finiteness from holonomicity of the associated module over the quantised character stack (Gabber-type integrability of characteristics).

This is why the status is **partially-solved**: the $SL_N$ specialisations are theorems; the two-variable module over $\mathbb{Q}(v,z)$ is not covered.

## 4. Partial Results / Verified Cases

| Manifold class | Result |
|---|---|
| $S^3$ | $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(S^3;R)\cong R$, free of rank $1$ on $\emptyset$ (equivalent to existence/uniqueness of the HOMFLYPT polynomial, 1985). |
| Handlebodies $H_g$, $g\ge1$; $F\times I$ | Free of countably infinite rank (Turaev 1988; Przytycki 1992) — finiteness **fails**, confirming the closed hypothesis is necessary. |
| $S^1\times S^2$ | Complete computation by Gilmer–Zhong (2001); finitely generated after passing to $\mathbb{Q}(v,z)$, with nontrivial torsion over $R$. |
| Connected sums | $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(M_1\\#M_2)$ is the tensor product over a localisation of $R$ (Gilmer–Zhong 2001): finiteness is multiplicative. |
| All closed oriented $M$, $G=SL_N$ or $GL_N$, generic $q$ | Finite-dimensional (Gunningham–Jordan–Safronov 2021). The theorem is stated for skein modules attached to $\mathrm{Rep}_q(G)$ for reductive $G$ *(scope for general $G$ — verify against the published statement)*. |
| $N=2$, i.e. Kauffman bracket | Finite dimensionality known for all closed $M$; explicit bases known for $\Sigma\times S^1$ (Detcherry–Wolff 2021), lens spaces, prism manifolds and small Seifert fibered spaces (Hoste–Przytycki; Mroczkowski; Detcherry–Kalfagianni–Sikora). |
| Torsion | Infinite $\mathbb{Z}[A^{\pm1}]$-torsion in $\mathcal{S}_{2,\infty}(S^1\times S^2)$ (Hoste–Przytycki 1995), so the integral form of the conjecture is false as stated for $N=2$; the HOMFLYPT analogue is expected to fail likewise. |

## 5. Principal Obstacles

- **No uniform diagrammatic algorithm.** Handle-slide relations depend on a surgery presentation; different presentations of the same $M$ give unrelated-looking presentations of the module, and there is no known normal form ("finitely many reduced diagrams") for links in a general closed $M$. Diagrammatic proofs succeed only where an explicit basis of the complement's skein module plus few slides can be managed by hand (lens spaces, $S^1\times S^2$).
- **Two variables versus one.** The GJS machinery quantises the $G$-character stack for a *fixed* reductive $G$. The HOMFLYPT module is a single object over $\mathbb{Q}(v,z)$ whose specialisations $v=q^N$ recover all $N$ at once. Finite dimension for every $N$ carries no formal implication for the generic-$v$ module unless the dimensions are bounded uniformly in $N$ **and** a flatness/base-change statement is available; neither is known.
- **Non-semisimplicity and infinite rank of the input category.** The HOMFLYPT category (the "skein category" of the Hecke algebras $H_n(q)$ with $v$ generic) has objects indexed by all partitions and is not the representation category of a fixed quantum group; the finite-generation arguments in factorization homology use finiteness properties of $\mathrm{Rep}_q(G)$ that this category lacks.
- **Torsion is not controlled.** Over $R$ the module is not Noetherian-tame: the known torsion in the $N=2$ case is an infinite direct sum, so any integral statement requires understanding the divisors $1-v^{2}z^{k}$-type elements that appear, and no structural theory of these exists.
- **Character-variety heuristics are only a shadow.** $\dim_{\mathbb{C}}\mathcal{O}(X_{SL_N}(\pi_1 M))$ can be infinite when the character variety is positive-dimensional, yet the quantum module is finite-dimensional; the deformation is not flat, so classical finiteness neither implies nor is implied by the quantum statement.

## 6. The Gap

Proven: finiteness for each $SL_N$-specialisation at generic $q$, plus complete computations for $S^3$, $S^1\times S^2$ and connected sums thereof. Conjectured: finiteness of the single two-variable module $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(M;\mathbb{Q}(v,z))$ for all closed $M$.

The precise missing step is a **generic-$v$ holonomicity theorem**: an interpretation of the HOMFLYPT skein module as the global sections of a holonomic module over a quantisation of a "$GL_\infty$" or Hecke-type character stack, valid with $v$ transcendental rather than $v=q^N$. Equivalently, one needs either (i) a uniform bound $\dim \mathcal{S}_{SL_N}(M)\le C(M)$ independent of $N$ plus a base-change argument, or (ii) a purely skein-theoretic finite spanning set for closed $M$ obtained from a surgery presentation. Neither exists even for a single closed hyperbolic $3$-manifold.

## 7. Current Research (as of June 2026)

- **Factorization-homology school** (Edinburgh/Jordan, Hamburg, Zurich, Melbourne): extending the GJS argument beyond $\mathrm{Rep}_q(G)$ to skein categories of Hecke type, using Ben-Zvi–Brochier–Jordan quantum character stacks. The target is a HOMFLYPT-level statement with $v$ generic. *(frontier — verify)*
- **Elliptic Hall algebra methods** (Morton, Samuelson, and successors): the torus skein algebra structure gives explicit action-of-$SL_2(\mathbb{Z})$ tools for Dehn fillings and torus bundles, the most promising route to explicit finite bases for infinite families. *(frontier — verify)*
- **Explicit computation programme** (Przytycki school; Bakshi, Ibarra, Montoya-Vega, Mroczkowski; Detcherry, Kalfagianni, Sikora): bases and torsion for Kauffman bracket and Dubrovnik modules of Seifert fibered spaces, with HOMFLYPT analogues attempted for lens spaces $L(p,1)$.
- **Dimension formulas.** Beyond mere finiteness, the expectation that $\dim \mathcal{S}(M)$ at generic $q$ equals a count attached to the character variety (e.g. the number of irreducible components / an Euler characteristic) is actively tested. *(frontier — verify)*

## 8. Future Work

1. **Uniformity in $N$.** Prove $\dim_{\mathbb{C}(q)}\mathcal{S}_{SL_N}(M)$ is bounded independently of $N$ for a fixed closed $M$, then deduce the $\mathbb{Q}(v,z)$-statement by a specialisation/semicontinuity argument.
2. **Skein categories with Hecke input.** Develop factorization homology for the HOMFLYPT skein category directly, identifying the relevant "character stack" as a quantisation of the space of $\mathbb{Z}$-graded local systems.
3. **A surgery-theoretic finiteness lemma.** Show that for any surgery presentation, the ideal generated by handle slides contains, for each winding class, a monic relation in the Turaev generators $x_i$ — this would give a finite spanning set by a Gröbner-type argument.
4. **Torsion classification.** Determine the $R$-torsion of $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(M;R)$ for $S^1\times S^2$ and lens spaces and conjecture its general form (a "$q$-analogue of $H_1$" in Przytycki's sense).
5. **Computer verification.** Machine-assisted skein reduction for surgeries on small-crossing links, to certify finite spanning sets for the first few hundred closed manifolds.

## 10. Worked Example / Concrete Special Case

**(a) $M=S^3$: rank one.** Every framed link diagram can be reduced to $\emptyset$ using only the two relations. Induct on crossing number and unknotting number: the relation $v^{-1}L_+ - vL_- = zL_0$ lets one change any crossing at the cost of a diagram with fewer crossings ($L_0$), and an unknotted framed component is removed by the framing relation plus $\delta=(v^{-1}-v)/z$. Hence $\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(S^3;R)=R\cdot\emptyset$.

Concretely, take $P(\text{unknot})=1$ and compute the positive Hopf link $H$: applying the relation at one crossing gives $L_-=$ 2-component unlink, $L_0=$ unknot, so
$$v^{-1}P(H)-v\cdot\frac{v^{-1}-v}{z}=z\ \Longrightarrow\ P(H)=vz+\frac{v-v^{3}}{z}.$$
Now the right-handed trefoil $T$: changing one crossing gives $L_-=$ unknot, $L_0=H$, so
$$v^{-1}P(T)-v = z\,P(H)=vz^{2}+v-v^{3},$$
$$P(T)=v\big(vz^{2}+2v-v^{3}\big)=2v^{2}-v^{4}+v^{2}z^{2},$$
the standard HOMFLYPT polynomial of the trefoil. Each link is a single scalar multiple of $\emptyset$: the module is $1$-dimensional.

**(b) $M=S^1\times S^2$: infinite rank collapses.** Present $S^1\times S^2$ as $0$-surgery on the unknot $U\subset S^3$. The complement is a solid torus $V$, and by Turaev
$$\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(V;R)=R[x_i: i\neq 0],$$
free of countably infinite rank — the generators $x_1,x_2,x_3,\dots$ are the $(1,n)$-curves on the boundary torus pushed inside. Then
$$\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(S^1\times S^2)=\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(V)\big/\langle\, y-\mathrm{sl}(y)\ :\ y\in \mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{H}}(V)\,\rangle,$$
where $\mathrm{sl}$ slides a strand over the $0$-framed $2$-handle. Sliding the core curve $x_1$ over the handle returns a curve isotopic to $x_1$ but with a full twist inserted; expanding that twist by the skein relation expresses the difference as a polynomial in $x_1$ with coefficients in $R$, producing a relation of the schematic form
$$\big(1-v^{a}\big)x_1 \;=\; z\cdot(\text{lower-degree terms}),$$
and analogous relations in each winding degree $n$. Over $R$ the factors $1-v^{a}$ are non-units, so infinitely many torsion summands survive — exactly the phenomenon Hoste–Przytycki found for the Kauffman bracket, where
$$\mathcal{S}_{2,\infty}(S^1\times S^2)\cong\mathbb{Z}[A^{\pm1}]\ \oplus\ \bigoplus_{i\ge1}\mathbb{Z}[A^{\pm1}]/(1-A^{2i+4}).$$
Over $\mathbb{Q}(v,z)$ those factors become invertible, every $x_n$ is expressed in lower-degree generators, and the module becomes finite-dimensional. Gilmer–Zhong's computation carries this out for HOMFLYPT. The conjecture asserts that this collapse — a unit appearing in front of the top generator in every degree — happens for *every* closed $3$-manifold; that is precisely what no general argument yet delivers over $\mathbb{Q}(v,z)$.
