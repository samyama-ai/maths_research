# Jones Polynomial Detecting the Unknot

> **Topic:** Topology & Knot Theory · **ID:** `04-topology/jones-polynomial-detecting-the-unknot` · **Status:** open

## 1. Problem Statement / Conjecture

Let $K \subset S^3$ be a tame knot (a single smoothly embedded circle) and let $V_K(t) \in \mathbb{Z}[t^{\pm 1}]$ be its Jones polynomial, normalized so that $V_{\bigcirc}(t) = 1$.

**Question (Jones, 1985).** Does $V_K(t) = 1$ imply that $K$ is the unknot?

An affirmative answer ("the Jones unknot conjecture") requires a proof valid for all knots. A disproof requires exhibiting a single knot $K$ with $V_K(t) = 1$ together with a certificate of nontriviality — e.g. a nonabelian representation $\pi_1(S^3 \setminus K) \to SL_2(\mathbb{C})$, nonzero knot Floer homology in top Alexander grading, or reduced Khovanov homology of rank $> 1$.

Two constraints sharpen the statement:

- The **link** version is **false**. Thistlethwaite (2001) found a 2-component 15-crossing link with the Jones polynomial of the 2-component unlink; Eliahou–Kauffman–Thistlethwaite (2003) produced infinite families with $2$ and $3$ components. So any proof for knots must use connectivity, not just the skein relation.
- The problem is a *detection* question, not a *classification* question: distinct nontrivial knots (e.g. $5_1$ and the $(2,5)$-torus knot's mutants, or the Kanenobu families) routinely share Jones polynomials.

## 2. Mathematical Foundations

**Skein definition.** $V$ is the unique invariant of oriented links with $V_{\bigcirc} = 1$ satisfying
$$t^{-1} V_{L_+}(t) - t\, V_{L_-}(t) + \left(t^{-1/2} - t^{1/2}\right) V_{L_0}(t) = 0,$$
where $L_+, L_-, L_0$ differ at a single crossing (positive, negative, oriented smoothing).

**Kauffman bracket state sum.** For an unoriented diagram $D$ with crossing set $\mathcal{C}$, $|\mathcal{C}| = n$, a *state* is $\sigma: \mathcal{C} \to \{+1,-1\}$; write $|\sigma|$ for the number of loops in the resulting flat diagram $D_\sigma$ and $\sigma(D) = \sum_c \sigma(c)$. Then
$$\langle D \rangle = \sum_{\sigma} A^{\sigma(D)} \left(-A^2 - A^{-2}\right)^{|\sigma| - 1}, \qquad \langle \bigcirc \rangle = 1 .$$
With writhe $w(D)$,
$$V_L(t) = \left.\left(-A^{3}\right)^{-w(D)} \langle D \rangle \right|_{A = t^{-1/4}} .$$

**Algebraic origin.** $V$ arises from the Markov trace on the Temperley–Lieb algebra $TL_n(\delta)$, $\delta = -A^2 - A^{-2}$, generated by $e_1,\dots,e_{n-1}$ with
$$e_i^2 = \delta e_i, \quad e_i e_{i\pm 1} e_i = e_i, \quad e_i e_j = e_j e_i \ (|i-j| \ge 2),$$
via the representation $\rho: B_n \to TL_n$, $\rho(\sigma_i) = A + A^{-1} e_i$. Equivalently $V$ is the $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ quantum invariant at the fundamental representation.

**Categorification.** Khovanov homology $\mathit{Kh}^{i,j}(K)$ satisfies
$$\sum_{i,j} (-1)^i q^j \dim \mathit{Kh}^{i,j}(K) = (q + q^{-1})\, V_K(q^2),$$
so $V$ is a graded Euler characteristic: rank cancellation in $\mathit{Kh}$ is exactly the room in which a counterexample could hide.

**Known evaluations.** $V_K(1) = 1$, $|V_K(-1)| = \det(K) = |\Delta_K(-1)|$, $V_K(e^{2\pi i/3}) = 1$, $|V_K(i)| = 1$, $|V_K(e^{\pi i /3})| = (\sqrt 3)^{\,\dim H_1(\Sigma_2(K);\mathbb{Z}/3)}$ (Lickorish–Millett). Hence $V_K = 1$ forces $\det(K) = 1$ and $H_1(\Sigma_2(K);\mathbb{Z}/3) = 0$.

## 3. History & State of the Art (SOTA)

- **1984–85.** Jones discovers $V$ via type $\mathrm{II}_1$ subfactors and Hecke algebra representations of $B_n$; he raises the detection question in the same period. It reappears as Problem 1 in Jones's "Ten Problems" (2000) and in Kirby's low-dimensional topology problem list.
- **1987.** Kauffman's bracket gives an elementary state model; Kauffman, Murasugi and Thistlethwaite independently prove the Tait conjectures, showing $\operatorname{span} V_D = c(D)$ for reduced alternating diagrams — the first large class where detection follows.
- **1988.** Lickorish–Thistlethwaite extend the span bound to *adequate* links.
- **1997.** Dasbach–Hougardy check by computer that no nontrivial knot with at most 16 crossings has trivial Jones polynomial.
- **2001–03.** Thistlethwaite and then Eliahou–Kauffman–Thistlethwaite kill the link version with explicit tangle-surgery constructions.
- **2002.** Bigelow reduces the knot case to a braid-group question: if the Burau representation of $B_4$ is unfaithful, then $V$ fails to detect the unknot.
- **2011.** Kronheimer–Mrowka prove reduced Khovanov homology is an unknot detector, using a spectral sequence to singular instanton knot homology. This is the strongest positive evidence: only Euler-characteristic cancellation separates $V$ from a proven detector.
- **2018–21.** Tuzun–Sikora verify the conjecture computationally to 22 and then 24 crossings.

## 4. Partial Results / Verified Cases

Detection is **proved** for:

- **Alternating knots.** For a reduced alternating diagram with $n$ crossings, $\operatorname{span} V = n$; $V = 1$ forces $n = 0$ (Kauffman–Murasugi–Thistlethwaite, 1987).
- **Adequate knots** (in particular semi-adequate ones, via the extreme coefficients $\pm 1$ at the two ends of the state sum): $\operatorname{span} V = c(K)$ (Lickorish–Thistlethwaite, 1988).
- **Positive braid closures / positive knots.** The minimal degree of $V$ equals the Seifert genus $g > 0$ for closures of positive braids (Cromwell), so $V \ne 1$.
- **Closed 3-braids.** The Burau representation of $B_3$ is faithful (Magnus–Peluso), and the $TL_3$ trace separates the trivial class; combined with Birman–Menasco's classification of links that are closed 3-braids, no nontrivial closed 3-braid has $V = 1$.
- **Khovanov-thin knots.** If $\mathit{Kh}(K)$ is supported on two diagonals, Lee's spectral sequence forces $\operatorname{rank} \widetilde{\mathit{Kh}}(K) = \det(K)$; with $\det(K) = |V_K(-1)| = 1$ and Kronheimer–Mrowka, $K$ is unknotted. This covers all quasi-alternating knots.
- **Computational range.** No nontrivial knot with $\le 24$ crossings has $V = 1$ (Dasbach–Hougardy to 16; Tuzun–Sikora to 22 in 2018, to 24 in 2021 *(frontier — verify the 24-crossing tabulation independently)*). Tuzun–Sikora also verified the analogous HOMFLY-PT statement in comparable ranges.

Detection **fails** for links: 2- and 3-component counterexamples exist in infinite families (Eliahou–Kauffman–Thistlethwaite, 2003), the smallest known having 15 crossings.

## 5. Principal Obstacles

- **No geometric interpretation.** $V$ is defined diagrammatically or via quantum algebra; there is no established formula for $V$ in terms of $\pi_1(S^3\setminus K)$, the Alexander module, or a minimal genus surface. The volume conjecture and AJ conjecture propose such bridges but are themselves open.
- **Skein recursions do not see connectivity.** Every proof technique built on the skein relation or the bracket applies verbatim to links, where the statement is false. Any successful argument must inject a knot-specific input.
- **Tangle surgery preserves $V$ but not component count.** The Eliahou–Kauffman–Thistlethwaite machine replaces a tangle by one with the same Kauffman-bracket "vector" in $TL_2$; the operation cannot be steered to keep the result connected, and no controlled version producing knots is known.
- **Euler-characteristic cancellation.** Kronheimer–Mrowka give $\operatorname{rank}\widetilde{\mathit{Kh}}(K) > 1$ for nontrivial $K$, but a trivial $V$ only says the *signed* count cancels. Bounding cancellation requires control of the $\delta$-grading spread, available only for thin or nearly thin knots.
- **Burau at $n=4$.** Bigelow's reduction turns the problem into faithfulness of an explicit $3\times 3$ matrix representation of $B_4$ over $\mathbb{Z}[t^{\pm1}]$. Unfaithfulness is known for $n \ge 5$ (Bigelow 1999; earlier Moody, Long–Paton), but the $n=4$ case has resisted both algebraic (Lawrence–Krammer style) and computational search.
- **Search-space growth.** Knot tabulation scales roughly exponentially (about $10^9$ prime knots at 24 crossings); brute-force verification cannot go far beyond current bounds, and no heuristic predicts where a counterexample would sit.

## 6. The Gap

Proven: $V$ detects the unknot on classes where the state sum has no cancellation at its extreme degrees (alternating, adequate, positive), on homologically thin knots, and on the finite tabulated range $c(K) \le 24$. Also proven: the *categorified* invariant detects the unknot unconditionally.

Missing: a bound of the form
$$\operatorname{rank}\widetilde{\mathit{Kh}}(K) \le \Phi\!\left(\text{coefficients of } V_K\right)$$
valid for all knots, which with Kronheimer–Mrowka would close the problem instantly. Equivalently, one needs either (a) a lower bound on $\operatorname{span} V$ or on some evaluation of $V$ by a geometric quantity ($g(K)$, $b_1$, hyperbolic volume) for *all* knots, or (b) a construction converting the known multi-component counterexamples into a connected one — which would settle it negatively. The $B_4$ Burau question is a concrete, fully finite formulation of route (b).

## 7. Current Research (as of June 2026)

- **Categorified obstructions.** Extending unknot/low-crossing detection results — Kronheimer–Mrowka (unknot), Baldwin–Sivek (trefoils, Duke 2022), Baldwin–Dowlin–Levine–Lidman–Vazquez (figure eight) — toward effective rank bounds in terms of $V$. Groups at Princeton, Boston College, MIT, Cambridge.
- **Instanton and Floer-theoretic input.** Framed instanton and sutured invariants give genus detection; the aim is a genus-vs-$V$-degree inequality beyond the fibered/positive cases *(frontier — verify)*.
- **Braid-group approach.** Continued computational and algebraic attacks on faithfulness of Burau for $B_4$, including searches over kernel candidates via the Lawrence–Krammer representation and via $TL_4$ trace vanishing.
- **Tangle-replacement engineering.** Refinements of the Eliahou–Kauffman–Thistlethwaite construction, including "Jones-trivial" satellites and Brunnian-type families, seeking a connectivity-preserving variant.
- **Machine-assisted search.** Reinforcement-learning and SAT-based generation of large candidate knots with strongly cancelling bracket state sums *(frontier — verify)*; so far these produce near-misses (small nonzero $V - 1$) rather than counterexamples.
- **Quantum-topological reformulation.** Attempts to read $V$ off the $SL_2(\mathbb{C})$ character variety via the AJ conjecture, which would give the missing geometric interpretation.

## 8. Future Work

- Prove $\operatorname{rank}\widetilde{\mathit{Kh}}(K) \le C \cdot (\text{number of nonzero coefficients of } V_K) + 1$, or any cancellation bound with a nontrivial dependence on $V$.
- Settle faithfulness of the Burau representation of $B_4$; either answer is decisive for Bigelow's dichotomy.
- Determine whether every knot with $\det(K) = 1$ and trivial Alexander polynomial (e.g. Kinoshita–Terasaka and Conway families, where $V \ne 1$) obeys a uniform lower bound on $\operatorname{span} V$.
- Push tabulation-based verification past 25–26 crossings using bracket-hashing and distributed computation.
- Search for Jones-trivial *knots* among satellites and Whitehead doubles of the known Jones-trivial links.

## 10. Worked Example / Concrete Special Case

**Right-handed trefoil $3_1$ via the Kauffman bracket.** Take the standard closed 2-braid-plus-one diagram $D$ with $3$ positive crossings, $w(D) = +3$.

First, the Hopf link diagram $H$ (2 crossings): its four states give loop counts $1,2,2,1$ with $A$-exponents $2,0,0,-2$, so
$$\langle H \rangle = A^2\delta + 2A^0 + A^{-2}\delta \Big|_{\text{normalized}} = -A^4 - A^{-4}, \qquad \delta = -A^2 - A^{-2}.$$

Now resolve one crossing of $D$. The $A$-smoothing yields $H$; the $A^{-1}$-smoothing yields an unknot diagram with two negative kinks, contributing $(-A^{-3})^2 = A^{-6}$ by Reidemeister I. Hence
$$\langle D \rangle = A\left(-A^4 - A^{-4}\right) + A^{-1}\cdot A^{-6} = -A^{5} - A^{-3} + A^{-7}.$$

Normalize:
$$f_D = (-A^3)^{-3}\langle D\rangle = -A^{-9}\left(-A^5 - A^{-3} + A^{-7}\right) = A^{-4} + A^{-12} - A^{-16},$$
and substituting $A = t^{-1/4}$:
$$V_{3_1}(t) = -t^{4} + t^{3} + t \neq 1 .$$

**What a counterexample must do.** Checks: $V(1) = -1+1+1 = 1$ ✓; $\det(3_1) = |V(-1)| = |-1-1-1| = 3$, matching $\Delta_{3_1}(-1) = -3$. For a hypothetical nontrivial $K$ with $V_K = 1$ we would need $\det(K) = 1$, $H_1(\Sigma_2(K);\mathbb{Z}/3) = 0$, $K$ non-alternating, non-adequate, not a positive-braid closure, not a closed 3-braid, $c(K) \ge 25$, and — by Kronheimer–Mrowka — $\operatorname{rank}\widetilde{\mathit{Kh}}(K) \ge 3$ with all higher-rank generators cancelling in pairs of opposite homological parity and equal $q$-grading. In the trefoil, $\widetilde{\mathit{Kh}}$ has rank $3$ supported in homological degrees $0,2,3$ with no such cancellation; the conjecture asserts that this rigidity persists for every knot.
