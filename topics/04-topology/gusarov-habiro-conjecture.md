# Gusarov-Habiro Conjecture

> **Topic:** Topology & Knot Theory · **ID:** `04-topology/gusarov-habiro-conjecture` · **Status:** partially-solved

## 1. Problem Statement / Conjecture

Gusarov (Goussarov) and Habiro independently proposed that finite-type (Vassiliev) invariants of a fixed degree bound should be *exactly* the invariants of a geometric filtration defined by local surgery moves. In its two standard forms:

**(A) Link/knot version.** Let $L, L'$ be oriented links in $S^3$ with the same number of components. Then
$$L \ \sim_{C_n}\ L' \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad v(L) = v(L') \ \text{ for every finite-type invariant } v \text{ of degree } < n,$$
where $\sim_{C_n}$ denotes $C_n$-equivalence: the equivalence relation generated by ambient isotopy and $C_n$-moves (surgery along a simple tree clasper with $n$ leaves; equivalently, Gusarov's $n$-variation).

**(B) 3-manifold version (the form usually called *the* Gusarov–Habiro conjecture).** Let $M, M'$ be closed oriented 3-manifolds (or integral homology spheres). Then
$$M \ \sim_{Y_n}\ M' \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad v(M) = v(M') \ \text{ for every Goussarov–Habiro finite-type invariant of degree} < n,$$
where $\sim_{Y_n}$ is generated by surgery along connected graph claspers ("$Y$-graphs", "clovers") of degree $n$.

The implication $\Rightarrow$ is a theorem in both settings: $C_n$- (resp. $Y_n$-) equivalence preserves all invariants of degree $<n$. The content of the conjecture is the converse: that degree-$<n$ invariants form a **complete** set of invariants for the geometric filtration. Statement (A) is a theorem for knots in $S^3$ and open for links in general; statement (B) is open for $n \ge 4$ over $\mathbb{Z}$. A complete resolution requires either a clasper-calculus proof that the degree-$<n$ invariants separate $\sim_{C_n}$ (resp. $\sim_{Y_n}$) classes, or an explicit pair of objects sharing all degree-$<n$ invariants but lying in distinct classes.

## 2. Mathematical Foundations

**Finite-type invariants.** A knot invariant $v$ with values in an abelian group extends to singular knots by the Vassiliev skein relation
$$v(K_\times) \;=\; v(K_+) - v(K_-).$$
$v$ has *degree* $\le d$ if $v$ vanishes on all knots with $d+1$ double points. Let $\mathcal{V}_d$ denote the space of such invariants. The Kontsevich integral gives, over $\mathbb{Q}$, an isomorphism of $\bigcup_d \mathcal{V}_d$ with the graded dual of the space $\mathcal{A}$ of Jacobi diagrams modulo $\mathrm{AS}$ and $\mathrm{IHX}$ relations.

**Claspers.** A *clasper* $G \subset S^3$ is a compact surface built from disk-leaves, edges and nodes, embedded so that surgery along an associated framed link is defined. A *tree clasper of degree $n$* has $n$ leaves; surgery along a simple tree clasper of degree $n$ disjoint from a link $L$ is a **$C_n$-move**. Write $\mathcal{K}$ for the monoid of knots; then
$$\mathcal{K} = \mathcal{K}/{\sim_{C_1}} \twoheadleftarrow \cdots \ \text{ and } \ \mathcal{K}/{\sim_{C_{n+1}}} \twoheadrightarrow \mathcal{K}/{\sim_{C_n}}$$
is a tower of surjections; Habiro shows $\mathcal{K}/{\sim_{C_n}}$ is a finitely generated abelian group under connected sum.

**$Y$-surgery.** A $Y$-graph (Borromean/clover surgery) is a degree-1 graph clasper with three leaves; surgery on it does not change $H_1$ or the linking form. $Y_n$-equivalence is generated by surgery on connected claspers with $n$ nodes. For 3-manifolds, finite-type invariants are defined by the Goussarov–Habiro filtration: $v$ has degree $\le d$ if the alternating sum
$$\sum_{S \subseteq \{G_1,\dots,G_{d+1}\}} (-1)^{|S|}\, v\!\left(M_S\right) \;=\; 0$$
vanishes for every $(d+1)$-tuple of disjoint $Y$-graphs, $M_S$ being surgery on the sub-family $S$.

**Key theorem (Goussarov 1999; Habiro 2000).** For knots $K, K' \subset S^3$:
$$K \sim_{C_n} K' \iff v(K)=v(K') \ \ \forall\, v \in \mathcal{V}_{n-1}.$$
Consequently $\mathcal{K}/{\sim_{C_n}}$ is isomorphic to the group of degree-$<n$ "universal" values, and the associated graded of the $C_n$-filtration is computed by Jacobi diagrams over $\mathbb{Q}$.

**Milnor invariants.** For an $m$-component link, Milnor's $\bar\mu$-invariants of length $\le n$ are invariants of $C_n$-equivalence, and length-$n$ Milnor invariants of string links are finite-type of degree $n-1$ (Bar-Natan; Lin; Habegger–Masbaum). They are the principal computable obstructions in the link case.

## 3. History & State of the Art (SOTA)

- **1987.** Matveev proves that two closed oriented 3-manifolds are Borromean-surgery ($Y_1$-) equivalent iff they have isomorphic $H_1$ together with isomorphic linking forms. This is case $n=1$ of (B).
- **1989.** Murakami–Nakanishi prove that links are $\Delta$-move ($C_2$-) equivalent iff all pairwise linking numbers agree — case $n=2$ of (A).
- **1990s.** Vassiliev and Goussarov introduce finite-type invariants; Bar-Natan formalizes the diagram algebra. Goussarov develops "$n$-equivalence" and the geometric technique of variations; Habiro independently develops clasper calculus in his thesis.
- **1999–2001.** Goussarov (C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris, 1999) and Habiro (*Geometry & Topology*, 2000) publish the knot theorem and announce the general programme; Garoufalidis–Goussarov–Polyak give the "calculus of clovers" for 3-manifolds, establishing that $Y_n$-equivalence preserves degree-$<n$ invariants and computing the graded pieces over $\mathbb{Q}$.
- **2003–2010.** Massuyeau–Meilhan classify $Y_2$-equivalence for homology cylinders; Auclair–Lescop reprove and refine clover calculus for homology spheres; Meilhan–Yasuhara push the link case into degree $<5$ for string links.
- **2008.** Massuyeau links the integral 3-manifold conjecture to the **dimension subgroup problem** for finitely generated groups, showing that the rational statement holds while the integral statement is entangled with torsion phenomena of the same type that make $D_4(G) \ne \gamma_4(G)$ possible (Rips).

**SOTA summary:** the rational/graded statement is understood; the integral, unstable statement is proven only in low degrees or for special classes.

## 4. Partial Results / Verified Cases

- **Knots in $S^3$: fully proven, all $n$** (Goussarov; Habiro). $\mathcal{K}/\sim_{C_n}$ is a finitely generated abelian group detected exactly by $\mathcal{V}_{n-1}$.
- **Links, $n = 2$:** $\Delta$-equivalence $=$ linking numbers (Murakami–Nakanishi 1989).
- **Links, $n = 3$:** clasp-pass classification via linking numbers, $\bar\mu(ijk)$, and Casson-type coefficients (Taniyama–Yasuhara; Meilhan).
- **String links, degree $< 5$:** Meilhan–Yasuhara characterize all finite-type string-link invariants of degree $\le 4$ and obtain the corresponding $C_n$-classification for $n \le 5$, confirming (A) in that range.
- **3-manifolds, $n=1$:** Matveev's theorem.
- **Integral homology spheres, $n=2$:** $Y_2$-classes are in bijection with $\mathbb{Z}$ via the Casson invariant $\lambda$, the unique degree-1 invariant.
- **$n = 3$ and homology cylinders:** Massuyeau–Meilhan's $Y_2$-classification of homology cylinders and Massuyeau's degree-3 results confirm the conjecture in these low-degree, restricted settings.
- **Rational coefficients / rational homology spheres:** the graded statement holds after $\otimes\,\mathbb{Q}$ (Garoufalidis–Goussarov–Polyak; Moussard for the $\mathbb{Q}$HS theory).
- **Grope filtration:** Conant–Teichner show the grope cobordism filtration of knots agrees with the $C_n$-filtration in the appropriate range, giving an independent geometric confirmation for knots.

## 5. Principal Obstacles

- **Torsion.** The Kontsevich integral, the one tool that inverts finite-type data geometrically, has denominators. Over $\mathbb{Q}$ the graded quotients $\mathcal{Y}_n/\mathcal{Y}_{n+1}$ are computed by Jacobi diagrams, but the conjecture is an *integral* statement; every known proof strategy loses exactly the torsion where a counterexample could hide.
- **No universal integral invariant for 3-manifolds.** For knots, Habiro's proof uses a universal $C_n$-invariant built by clasper surgery on a fixed "unknotting" model. No comparable finitely generated integral universal object is known for $\mathbb{Z}$HS's.
- **Non-abelian fundamental group.** For links and 3-manifolds the relevant algebra is the lower central series of $\pi_1$; Massuyeau's reduction shows the integral conjecture in degree $n$ implies (and is constrained by) the equality $D_n(G) = \gamma_n(G)$ of dimension subgroups. Rips's counterexample at $n=4$ shows this equality can fail for general groups, so any proof must exploit 3-manifold-specific structure rather than pure group theory.
- **Clasper moves are not confluent.** Clasper calculus supplies many relations (IHX, AS, slide) but no normal form; deciding whether two claspers give $C_n$-equivalent results is not algorithmic in known presentations.
- **Link case: basepoint/closure loss.** Milnor invariants are only well defined modulo indeterminacy for closed links; the string-link category where the theory is clean does not map bijectively onto links, so results for string links do not descend automatically.

## 6. The Gap

Proven: the forward implication in all degrees; the full equivalence for knots in $S^3$; degrees $n \le 5$ for string links; degrees $n \le 3$ for 3-manifolds; the rational graded statement in all degrees.

Missing: an **integral, unstable** surjectivity statement. Concretely, one must show that the natural map
$$\mathcal{Y}_n\big/\mathcal{Y}_{n+1} \;\longrightarrow\; \mathrm{Hom}\big(\mathcal{V}_n/\mathcal{V}_{n-1},\, \mathbb{Z}\big)$$
is injective over $\mathbb{Z}$, not merely after tensoring with $\mathbb{Q}$. Equivalently: rule out a pair $M, M'$ with all degree-$<n$ invariants equal that are separated only by a torsion class in the graded piece. For $n \ge 4$ this is where the dimension subgroup obstruction bites, and no technique currently distinguishes "torsion that is a genuine geometric obstruction" from "torsion killed by an unnoticed clasper relation".

## 7. Current Research (as of June 2026)

- **Clasper calculus and homology cylinders.** Groups around Massuyeau (Dijon), Meilhan (Grenoble) and Habiro (Kyoto/RIMS) continue to study the monoid of homology cylinders, the Johnson filtration and the $Y_n$-tower; results there feed directly into (B) via the mapping-class-group correspondence.
- **Integral homology-sphere invariants.** Refinements of the LMO invariant and Habiro's cyclotomic ring $\widehat{\mathbb{Z}[q]}$ are the leading candidates for an integral universal invariant; whether Habiro's unified WRT invariant is universal for the $Y_n$-filtration over $\mathbb{Z}$ is an active question *(frontier — verify)*.
- **Link case.** Extensions of Meilhan–Yasuhara's programme to degree 5–6 for string links and to closed links via self-$C_n$-equivalence and $C_n$-concordance *(frontier — verify)*.
- **Dimension subgroup interface.** Ongoing attempts to determine whether the groups arising as $\pi_1$ of 3-manifolds (or their nilpotent quotients) avoid Rips-type torsion, which would upgrade Massuyeau's rational result to $\mathbb{Z}$ in higher degrees.

## 8. Future Work

- Construct an explicit finitely generated integral universal $Y_n$-invariant of $\mathbb{Z}$HS's, mirroring Habiro's knot construction.
- Settle degree 4 for integral homology spheres — the first case where the dimension subgroup obstruction is nontrivial; a positive or negative answer there is expected to determine the shape of the general result.
- Compute $\mathcal{Y}_n/\mathcal{Y}_{n+1}$ over $\mathbb{Z}$ for small $n$ by machine, using clasper-move rewriting, to search for torsion classes not visible to finite-type invariants.
- Decide the link version for $n=6$ and identify whether closure indeterminacy of Milnor invariants creates a genuine counterexample.
- Relate the $C_n$/grope/Whitney-tower filtrations integrally (Conant–Schneiderman–Teichner) to import obstruction-theoretic torsion computations.

## 10. Worked Example / Concrete Special Case

**Knots up to $C_3$-equivalence.** Take $n = 3$ in statement (A) for knots. The space $\mathcal{V}_2$ of degree-$\le 2$ knot invariants is spanned by the constant $1$ and the second coefficient $a_2(K)$ of the Conway polynomial $\nabla_K(z) = 1 + a_2(K)z^2 + \cdots$. The Goussarov–Habiro theorem therefore predicts a bijection
$$\mathcal{K}/{\sim_{C_3}} \;\xrightarrow{\ \cong\ }\; \mathbb{Z}, \qquad [K] \mapsto a_2(K).$$

Check the pieces:

1. **$n=2$ first.** $a_2$ is degree 2, hence *not* an invariant of $C_2$-equivalence. Indeed the $\Delta$-move unknots every knot (Murakami–Nakanishi), so $\mathcal{K}/{\sim_{C_2}}$ is trivial — consistent with $\mathcal{V}_1 = \mathbb{Q}\cdot 1$.
2. **Values.** Trefoil $3_1$: $\nabla = 1 + z^2$, so $a_2 = 1$. Figure-eight $4_1$: $\nabla = 1 - z^2$, so $a_2 = -1$. Since $1 \ne -1$, the trefoil and figure-eight are **not** $C_3$-equivalent, though they *are* $C_2$-equivalent.
3. **Additivity and surjectivity.** $a_2(K \\# K') = a_2(K) + a_2(K')$, so $\\#^k 3_1$ has $a_2 = k$ and the mirror $\\#^k \overline{3_1}$ has $a_2 = -k$: every integer is realized, and the map is onto $\mathbb{Z}$.
4. **Injectivity — the conjectural content, here a theorem.** A single $C_3$-move changes $a_2$ by $\pm 1$ (the clasper of degree 2 realizes the unique degree-2 Jacobi diagram, the "theta" chord diagram, with coefficient $1$). Given $a_2(K) = a_2(K')$, one shows $K \\# \overline{K'}$ has $a_2 = 0$ and clasper calculus contracts it to the unknot through $C_3$-moves, giving $K \sim_{C_3} K'$.

**The analogous 3-manifold case.** For integral homology spheres with $n = 2$: degree-$\le 1$ invariants are spanned by $1$ and the Casson invariant $\lambda$, and $Y_2$-classes are in bijection with $\mathbb{Z}$ via $\lambda$; a single $Y_1$-surgery changes $\lambda$ by $\pm 1$, e.g. Poincaré sphere $\Sigma(2,3,5)$ has $\lambda = -1$ against $\lambda(S^3)=0$. The difficulty appears at $n = 4$, where the target group of degree-3 data acquires torsion and no clasper contraction argument of the above kind is known.
