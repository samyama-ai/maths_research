# Gromov-Witten Invariants Integrality

> **Topic:** Topology & Knot Theory · **ID:** `04-topology/gromov-witten-invariants-integrality` · **Status:** partially-solved

## 1. Problem Statement / Conjecture

Gromov–Witten (GW) invariants of a symplectic or projective manifold are **rational** numbers: they count stable maps as a virtual degree of a moduli space with $\mathbb{Q}$-coefficients, and multiple covers and orbifold automorphisms produce denominators. The Gopakumar–Vafa (GV) conjecture asserts that on a Calabi–Yau 3-fold this rationality is an artifact: the entire all-genus GW generating function is a universal transform of a table of **integers** $n^g_\beta \in \mathbb{Z}$ ("BPS counts"), only finitely many of which are nonzero for each curve class $\beta$.

Precisely, let $X$ be a closed Calabi–Yau 3-fold (complex, or symplectic with $c_1(TX)=0$). Let $N_{g,\beta}\in\mathbb{Q}$ be its genus-$g$, class-$\beta$ GW invariant. **Conjecture (Gopakumar–Vafa, 1998).** There exist integers $n^g_\beta$, zero for $g$ large given $\beta$, with

$$\sum_{g\ge 0}\ \sum_{\beta\neq 0} N_{g,\beta}\,\lambda^{2g-2} q^{\beta} \;=\; \sum_{g\ge 0}\ \sum_{\beta\neq 0}\ \sum_{k\ge 1} \frac{n^g_\beta}{k}\Big(2\sin\tfrac{k\lambda}{2}\Big)^{2g-2} q^{k\beta}.$$

A complete solution requires: (i) **integrality** — the $n^g_\beta$ defined by inverting this formula lie in $\mathbb{Z}$; (ii) **finiteness** — $n^g_\beta=0$ for $g\gg_\beta 0$; (iii) ideally, a **geometric definition** of $n^g_\beta$ as a count of something (sheaves, embedded curves, M2-branes) that is integral by construction and provably matches the GW side. A disproof would exhibit a Calabi–Yau 3-fold and a class $\beta$ with $n^g_\beta\notin\mathbb{Z}$ or with infinitely many nonzero genera.

The knot-theoretic avatar is the **LMOV conjecture** (Labastida–Mariño–Ooguri–Vafa): the HOMFLY-PT polynomials of a link, reorganized through the large-$N$ duality of Chern–Simons theory on $S^3$ with the resolved conifold, are generated by integers counting open BPS states.

## 2. Mathematical Foundations

**Moduli of stable maps.** For $X$ symplectic of dimension $6$ with $\omega$-compatible $J$, and $\beta\in H_2(X;\mathbb{Z})$, let $\overline{\mathcal M}_{g}(X,\beta)$ be the Kontsevich space of genus-$g$ stable maps. Its virtual dimension in the Calabi–Yau 3-fold case is
$$\operatorname{vdim}_{\mathbb R}\overline{\mathcal M}_g(X,\beta) \;=\; 2\big(c_1(TX)\cdot\beta + (\dim_{\mathbb C}X-3)(1-g)\big) \;=\; 0,$$
so $N_{g,\beta}=\deg\big[\overline{\mathcal M}_g(X,\beta)\big]^{\mathrm{vir}}\in\mathbb{Q}$ is a number. Rationality is forced by nontrivial automorphisms of multiple covers.

**The GV transform, unwound.** Writing $F(\lambda,q)=\sum N_{g,\beta}\lambda^{2g-2}q^\beta$, the transform is triangular in $g$ and in $\beta$ (with respect to the Mori cone partial order), so the $n^g_\beta$ are determined recursively over $\mathbb{Q}$. Genus $0$ gives the Aspinwall–Morrison / multiple-cover relation
$$N_{0,\beta}=\sum_{k\mid \beta} \frac{n^0_{\beta/k}}{k^{3}},\qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad n^0_\beta=\sum_{k\mid\beta}\frac{\mu(k)}{k^{3}}N_{0,\beta/k},$$
with $\mu$ the Möbius function; genus $1$ gives $N_{1,\beta}=\sum_{k\mid\beta}\big(\tfrac{1}{12}n^0_{\beta/k}\tfrac1k+n^1_{\beta/k}\tfrac{\sigma(k)}{k}\big)$-type identities. Integrality is therefore a nontrivial congruence statement on the rational numbers $N_{g,\beta}$, not a formal consequence.

**Useful expansion.** With $x=k\lambda$,
$$\big(2\sin(x/2)\big)^{-2}=\frac{1}{x^{2}}+\frac{1}{12}+\frac{x^{2}}{240}+\frac{x^{4}}{6048}+\cdots,\qquad \frac{|B_{2g}|}{2g\,(2g-2)!}=\text{coefficient of }x^{2g-2}.$$

**Sheaf-theoretic side.** Let $M_\beta$ be the moduli of one-dimensional stable sheaves $F$ on $X$ with $[\operatorname{supp}F]=\beta$, $\chi(F)=1$, with Hilbert–Chow map $\pi:M_\beta\to \mathrm{Chow}_\beta(X)$. Maulik–Toda define $n^g_\beta$ from the perverse sheaf of vanishing cycles $\phi_{M_\beta}$ via
$$\sum_{g} n^g_\beta \big(y^{1/2}+y^{-1/2}\big)^{2g}=\sum_{i}\chi\big({}^{p}\!\mathcal H^{i}(R\pi_*\phi_{M_\beta})\big)\,y^{i},$$
which is manifestly integral; equality with the GW-derived $n^g_\beta$ is the conjecture. Related integral theories: Donaldson–Thomas invariants and Pandharipande–Thomas stable-pair invariants $P_{n,\beta}\in\mathbb{Z}$, with the GW/PT correspondence converting GV integrality into a statement about the PT series.

## 3. History & State of the Art (SOTA)

- **1998.** Gopakumar and Vafa, in *M-theory and topological strings I, II*, propose the formula from an M2-brane/Schwinger-computation picture; $n^g_\beta$ counts BPS states graded by an $SU(2)_L$ Lefschetz action on moduli of D2-branes.
- **2000.** Ooguri–Vafa extend the picture to knots and open strings; Labastida–Mariño–Vafa formalize it into the LMOV integrality conjecture for HOMFLY-PT.
- **2001.** Bryan–Pandharipande give the first rigorous mathematical treatments in local geometries ("BPS states of curves in Calabi–Yau 3-folds"), verifying the transform for local curves.
- **2003–2009.** Topological vertex (Aganagic–Klemm–Mariño–Vafa) computes all-genus local toric amplitudes; Peng (2007) and Konishi (2008) turn this into proofs of GV integrality for large classes of toric Calabi–Yau 3-folds. MNOP conjecture ties GW to DT; PT theory (2009) supplies an integral counting theory.
- **2018.** Maulik–Toda define $n^g_\beta$ via vanishing cycles. Ionel–Parker prove the GV integrality statement for symplectic Calabi–Yau 6-manifolds by symplectic-geometric "cluster/local structure" arguments (Annals of Mathematics).
- **2021.** Doan–Ionel–Walpuski prove the **Gopakumar–Vafa finiteness** statement for generic almost complex structures, closing the finiteness half.
- **Status.** Integrality is a theorem in the symplectic category; the geometric interpretation and the algebraic-geometry-native statement (Maulik–Toda equality, and integrality for the *complex-structure* $J$ of a given Calabi–Yau) remain open.

## 4. Partial Results / Verified Cases

- **Genus 0, all Calabi–Yau 3-folds with a torus/localization handle:** the Aspinwall–Morrison multiple-cover formula gives $n^0_\beta\in\mathbb{Z}$ in every case computed; proven in general by the symplectic route.
- **Symplectic Calabi–Yau 6-manifolds:** Ionel–Parker (2018) prove $n^g_\beta\in\mathbb{Z}$ and $n^g_\beta=0$ for $g$ large, for generic tamed $J$; finiteness re-proved and strengthened by Doan–Ionel–Walpuski (2021).
- **Local toric Calabi–Yau 3-folds:** GV integrality proven for all classes via the topological vertex — Peng (2007) for local toric geometries, Konishi (2008) for a large family including $K_{\mathbb P^2}$, $K_{\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1}$, resolved conifold $\mathcal O(-1)^{\oplus2}\to\mathbb P^1$.
- **Local curves and local surfaces:** $\mathcal O(-1)\oplus\mathcal O(-1)\to\mathbb P^1$ (all $d,g$, exactly); local del Pezzo surfaces to high degree; $K3\times E$ and the Katz–Klemm–Vafa formula (proved by Pandharipande–Thomas, 2016) give integrality for the reduced $K3$ theory.
- **Quintic 3-fold $X_5\subset\mathbb P^4$:** genus 0 to degree $\ge 10$ (Candelas–de la Ossa–Green–Parkes and successors); genus 1 in all degrees via Zinger's proof of the BCOV genus-1 formula (2009); higher genus numerically integral into the $g\lesssim 50$, degree $\lesssim 10$ range from BCOV holomorphic-anomaly plus boundary data.
- **Knots:** LMOV integrality proven by Liu–Peng (2010) for the framed HOMFLY-PT setting; independently established for all knots admitting a knots–quivers correspondence (Kucharski–Reineke–Stošić–Sułkowski, 2017–2019), which covers all $2$-bridge knots and all torus knots.

## 5. Principal Obstacles

- **No integral count on the GW side.** $[\overline{\mathcal M}_g(X,\beta)]^{\mathrm{vir}}$ has $\mathbb{Q}$-coefficients intrinsically: stable maps with automorphisms sit on $\mathbb{Q}$-gerbes. There is no known modification of the virtual class making it $\mathbb{Z}$-valued, so integrality must come from a *comparison* with a different theory, not from within GW theory.
- **Excess and non-reduced structure.** Superabundant curves — a class $\beta$ whose curves move in families of the wrong dimension, or with embedded/nilpotent structure — make the local contribution of a curve to $N_{g,\beta}$ depend on more than its topology. Deformation to a generic $J$ solves this symplectically but is unavailable for a fixed algebraic Calabi–Yau.
- **Genus grows without an a priori bound.** Finiteness ($n^g_\beta=0$ for $g\gg0$) fails for naive definitions; the difficulty is controlling limits of $J$-holomorphic maps of unbounded genus with bounded area, where Gromov compactness gives no genus bound. Doan–Ionel–Walpuski's resolution is genuinely delicate and generic-$J$.
- **Wall-crossing.** The sheaf-side invariants (DT/PT/Maulik–Toda) depend on stability; showing invariance and matching with GW requires the full Joyce–Song / Kontsevich–Soibelman machinery, plus orientation data and a global-critical-chart hypothesis on $M_\beta$ that is not known in general.
- **Analysis vs. algebra mismatch.** The proofs available are symplectic and perturbative; the numbers of interest to enumerative geometers and physicists come from complex geometry and mirror symmetry, where no perturbation is allowed.

## 6. The Gap

Proven: for a *generic* almost complex structure on a symplectic Calabi–Yau 6-manifold, GW invariants have the GV form with integer, finitely-supported $n^g_\beta$ (Ionel–Parker; Doan–Ionel–Walpuski). Conjectured: the same integers are (a) intrinsically defined by the geometry of $X$ — independent of $J$ in a way that assigns a meaning to $n^g_\beta$ per curve class, and (b) computed by the sheaf-theoretic formula of Maulik–Toda. The exact missing step is a **GW $=$ sheaf-count theorem in fixed complex structure**: proving that the perverse-sheaf-theoretic $n^g_\beta$ of $M_\beta$ agree with the GW-derived ones. Equivalently, proving the full GW/PT correspondence for arbitrary Calabi–Yau 3-folds (known for toric, and for many complete intersections only in low genus or low degree) would transport integrality from $P_{n,\beta}\in\mathbb{Z}$ to $n^g_\beta$.

## 7. Current Research (as of June 2026)

- **Categorified/cohomological DT.** Extending Maulik–Toda to a globally defined perverse sheaf requires orientation data and gluing of critical charts; work in the Oxford (Joyce and collaborators) and Tokyo (Toda) circles continues on $(-1)$-shifted symplectic structures and Darboux-type theorems. *(frontier — verify)*
- **Higher-genus quintic.** Guo–Janda–Ruan's logarithmic/Mixed-Spin-P-field program, and the Chang–Li–Li–Liu NMSP approach, have produced structural results (finite generation, holomorphic anomaly) allowing higher-genus computations; integrality checks now extend into genus $\sim 60$ for the quintic. *(frontier — verify)*
- **Symplectic side.** Doan, Ionel and Walpuski's methods are being pushed toward non-generic $J$ and toward open/relative settings.
- **Knots–quivers.** Ongoing extension of the correspondence (Sułkowski's Warsaw group, Ekholm, Stošić) aims at LMOV integrality for all knots by exhibiting a quiver whose DT invariants are the BPS counts.

## 8. Future Work

- Prove the Maulik–Toda equality for a single non-toric compact Calabi–Yau (the quintic is the test case).
- Remove genericity: define $n^g_\beta$ for a fixed integrable complex structure and prove deformation invariance directly.
- Establish GW/PT in full generality; several groups treat this as the cleanest route to unconditional integrality.
- Prove LMOV for all knots via a general knots–quivers correspondence, and extend to links and to colored superpolynomials.
- Find effective bounds: an explicit function $G(\beta)$ with $n^g_\beta=0$ for $g>G(\beta)$, currently non-constructive.

## 10. Worked Example / Concrete Special Case

**Resolved conifold $X=\mathcal O(-1)\oplus\mathcal O(-1)\to\mathbb P^1$.** Its all-genus free energy is known in closed form (Faber–Pandharipande multiple-cover computation):
$$F(\lambda,Q)=\sum_{d\ge1}\frac{Q^{d}}{d\,\big(2\sin(d\lambda/2)\big)^{2}}.$$
Compare with the GV ansatz. The right-hand side is exactly the $g=0$, $\beta=[\mathbb P^1]$, $n^0_1=1$ term, with all other $n^g_\beta=0$. So the prediction is: **one integer, equal to $1$** — the single rigid rational curve $\mathbb P^1\subset X$ — reproduces infinitely many rational GW invariants.

Unwinding with $\big(2\sin(x/2)\big)^{-2}=x^{-2}+\tfrac1{12}+\tfrac{x^{2}}{240}+\cdots$ at $x=d\lambda$:
$$F=\sum_{d\ge1}Q^{d}\Big(\frac{1}{d^{3}\lambda^{2}}+\frac{1}{12\,d}+\frac{d\,\lambda^{2}}{240}+\cdots\Big),$$
so
$$N_{0,d}=\frac{1}{d^{3}},\qquad N_{1,d}=\frac{1}{12\,d},\qquad N_{2,d}=\frac{d}{240},\qquad N_{g,d}=\frac{|B_{2g}|}{2g\,(2g-2)!}\,d^{2g-3}.$$
Every one of these is a non-integer for $d\ge2$ (e.g. $N_{0,2}=1/8$, $N_{1,3}=1/36$, $N_{2,5}=1/48$), yet the Möbius inversion returns integers: $n^0_1=N_{0,1}=1$ and $n^0_2=N_{0,2}-\tfrac{1}{8}N_{0,1}=\tfrac18-\tfrac18=0$, $n^0_3=N_{0,3}-\tfrac{1}{27}N_{0,1}=\tfrac1{27}-\tfrac1{27}=0$, and similarly all higher-genus entries cancel.

**Contrast — the quintic $X_5\subset\mathbb P^4$.** Here $N_{0,1}=2875$, and
$$N_{0,2}=n^0_2+\frac{n^0_1}{8}=609250+\frac{2875}{8}=609609.375,$$
$$N_{0,3}=n^0_3+\frac{n^0_1}{27}=317206375+\frac{2875}{27}=317206481.481\ldots$$
The GW invariants are visibly rational with denominators $8$ and $27$; the conjecture says every such denominator is accounted for by the $1/k^3$ multiple-cover factor, leaving the integers $2875$ (lines), $609250$ (conics), $317206375$ (twisted cubics). No proof of this pattern exists for the quintic in fixed complex structure at arbitrary genus — precisely the gap of Section 6.
