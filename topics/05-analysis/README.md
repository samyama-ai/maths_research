# Real & Complex Analysis

Fourier analysis, operator algebras, complex dynamics, functional analysis, measure theory

## Problems Index

* 🟢 [Ahlfors Measure Conjecture](./ahlfors-measure-conjecture.md) — Limit sets of finitely generated Kleinian groups have zero measure.
* 🟢 [Alberti's Rank One Theorem](./alberti-rank-one.md) — Characterizes the singular part of derivative of bounded variation functions.
* 🟡 [AM-GM for Matrices](./am-gm-for-matrices.md) — Generalizes arithmetic and geometric means to positive definite matrices.
* 🔴 [Baez-Duarte Criterion](./baez-duarte-criterion.md) — Formulates the Riemann Hypothesis as a distance problem in Hardy spaces.
* 🔴 [Baker Conjecture](./baker-conjecture.md) — Entire functions of order less than half lack wandering domains.
* 🟠 [Berry Random Wave Model](./berry-random-wave-model.md) — High energy eigenfunctions behave like random plane waves.
* 🔴 [Beurling-Lax Theorem Generalizations](./beurling-lax-theorem-generalizations.md) — Characterization of invariant subspaces for operators on general Banach spaces.
* 🔴 [Beurling-Nyman Criterion](./beurling-nyman-criterion.md) — Equates the Riemann Hypothesis to a density problem in Hilbert space.
* 🟢 [Bieberbach Conjecture](./bieberbach-conjecture.md) — Coefficients of univalent functions are bounded by their index.
* 🟡 [Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer Analytic](./birch-and-swinnerton-dyer-analytic.md) — Rank of elliptic curves equals order of function at one.
* 🔴 [Bloch Conjecture](./bloch-conjecture.md) — Surfaces with zero geometric genus have trivial maps.
* 🔴 [Bochner-Riesz Conjecture](./bochner-riesz-conjecture.md) — Concerns the convergence of certain spherical partial sums.
* 🔴 [Borwein Conjecture](./borwein-conjecture.md) — Concerns the sign patterns in expansion of certain polynomial products.
* 🔴 [Bourgain Slicing Problem](./bourgain-slicing-problem.md) — Convex bodies have hyperplanes with large intersection volumes.
* 🟡 [Brascamp-Lieb Inequalities](./brascamp-lieb-constants.md) — Generalizes several classical inequalities by seeking their optimal bounding constants.
* 🔴 [Brennan Conjecture](./brennan-conjecture.md) — Bounds the integral of derivatives of conformal mappings.
* 🔴 [Calderon Zygmund Extensions](./calderon-zygmund-extensions.md) — Generalizations of singular integral operators to non homogeneous spaces.
* 🟢 [Carleson Conjecture](./carleson-conjecture.md) — Fourier series of square integrable functions converge almost everywhere.
* 🟡 [Chern-Ricci Flow](./chern-ricci-flow.md) — Evolution of Hermitian metrics by their Chern Ricci curvature.
* 🟢 [Connes Embedding Problem](./connes-embedding-problem.md) — Finite von Neumann algebras embed into the ultrapower of hyperfinite.
* 🟢 [Corona Theorem](./corona-theorem.md) — The open unit disk is dense in its maximal ideal space.
* 🟡 [De Giorgi Conjecture](./de-giorgi-conjecture.md) — Concerns the one dimensional symmetry of solutions to Allen Cahn equation.
* 🔴 [Density Hypothesis](./density-hypothesis.md) — Bounds the number of zeros of the Riemann zeta function.
* 🟢 [Hayman Conjecture](./hayman-conjecture.md) — An entire function and its derivative have common values.
* 🔴 [Ilin's Conjecture](./ilin-conjecture.md) — Investigates the uniform convergence of spectral expansions for general differential operators.
* 🔴 [Invariant Subspace Problem](./invariant-subspace-problem.md) — Every bounded operator on a Banach space has invariant subspaces.
* 🔴 [Iwaniec Conjecture](./iwaniec-conjecture.md) — Bounds the coefficients of univalent functions with real coefficients.
* 🟢 [Kadison-Singer Problem](./kadison-singer-problem.md) — Pure states on discrete groups extend uniquely to operators.
* 🟡 [Kahler-Ricci Flow Blowup](./kahler-ricci-flow-blowup.md) — Characterization of limits when flow encounters a singularity.
* 🔴 [Kakeya Conjecture](./kakeya-conjecture.md) — Sets containing line segments in all directions have maximal dimension.
* 🟢 [Kato's Root Problem](./kato-root-problem.md) — Addresses the domain of square roots of elliptic differential operators.
* 🟡 [Kazdan-Warner Problem](./kazdan-warner-problem.md) — Characterizes functions that can be scalar curvatures of metrics.
* 🔴 [Khavinson-Shapiro Conjecture](./khavinson-shapiro-conjecture.md) — Harmonic extensions of polynomials to ellipsoids are entire functions.
* 🟡 [KLS Conjecture](./kls-conjecture.md) — Isoperimetric constants of convex bodies are universally bounded.
* 🔴 [Kobayashi Hyperbolicity](./kobayashi-hyperbolicity.md) — Generic projective hypersurfaces of high degree are hyperbolic.
* 🔴 [Korevaar-Meyers Conjecture](./korevaar-meyers-conjecture.md) — Examines the Lipschitz regularity of harmonic functions on certain specific domains.
* 🟡 [KPZ Equation Analysis](./kpz-equation-analysis.md) — Mathematical rigorous formulation of the stochastic growth model.
* 🔴 [Kra Conjecture](./kra-conjecture.md) — Relates to the norm of the Poincare series operator.
* 🔴 [Krzyz Conjecture](./krzyz-conjecture.md) — Bounds the Taylor coefficients of nonvanishing bounded analytic functions.
* 🔴 [L-function Non-vanishing](./l-function-non-vanishing.md) — Central values of certain families of L functions are non zero.
* 🔴 [Lang Hyperbolic Varieties](./lang-hyperbolic-varieties.md) — Varieties are hyperbolic if and only if subvarieties are general type.
* 🔴 [Lehmer Conjecture](./lehmer-conjecture.md) — Mahler measure of non cyclotomic polynomials is strictly bounded away.
* 🟡 [Leopoldt Conjecture](./leopoldt-conjecture.md) — Non vanishing of the regulator for number fields.
* 🟡 [Lieb-Thirring Inequalities](./lieb-thirring-inequalities.md) — Sharp constants for bounds on negative eigenvalues of Schrodinger operators.
* 🔴 [Littlewood's Flat Polynomials Problem](./littlewood-flat-polynomials.md) — Questions the existence of polynomials with coefficients of absolute value one.
* 🟢 [Lusin's Return Times Theorem](./lusin-return-times.md) — Deals with pointwise convergence of ergodic averages in harmonic analysis.
* 🔴 [Mahler Volume Conjecture](./mahler-volume-conjecture.md) — Provides a strict lower bound for volume product of symmetric bodies.
* 🔴 [Mandelbrot Local Connectivity](./mandelbrot-local-connectivity.md) — The Mandelbrot set is locally connected everywhere.
* 🟢 [Marden Conjecture](./marden-conjecture.md) — Finitely generated Kleinian groups are topologically tame.
* 🟡 [Marstrand Projection Generalizations](./marstrand-projection-generalizations.md) — Projections of fractal sets retain maximal possible dimensions.
