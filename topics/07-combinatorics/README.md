# Combinatorics & Graph Theory

Ramsey theory, extremal graph theory, enumerative & algebraic combinatorics, combinatorial designs, matroid theory

## Problems Index

* 🔴 [1-Factorization Conjecture](./1-factorization-conjecture.md) — Regular graphs of high degree can be decomposed into perfect matchings.
* 🔴 [Aanderaa-Karp-Rosenberg Conjecture](./aanderaa-karp-rosenberg-conjecture.md) — Monotone graph properties require evaluating a certain number of adjacency matrix entries.
* 🔴 [Albertson Conjecture](./albertson-conjecture.md) — Complete graphs minimize the crossing number among all same chromatic graphs.
* 🔴 [Alon-Tarsi Conjecture](./alon-tarsi-conjecture.md) — Even and odd Eulerian subgraphs differ in bipartite graphs.
* 🟢 [Alspach's Conjecture](./alspachs-conjecture.md) — A complete graph can be completely decomposed into cycles of specified lengths.
* 🟢 [Alternating Sign Matrix Conjecture](./alternating-sign-matrix-conjecture.md) — Predicts the exact number of alternating sign matrices of a given size.
* 🔴 [Babai's Conjecture](./babais-conjecture.md) — Bounding the diameter of Cayley graphs of finite simple groups.
* 🔴 [Babai's Core-Free Graph Problem](./babais-core-free-graph-problem.md) — Bounding the size of core free graphs using their related automorphism groups.
* 🔴 [Barnette's Conjecture](./barnettes-conjecture.md) — Every bipartite cubic polyhedral graph is Hamiltonian.
* 🔴 [Berge-Fulkerson Conjecture](./berge-fulkerson-conjecture.md) — Bridgeless cubic graphs have six perfect matchings covering edges twice.
* 🔴 [Bermond-Thomassen Conjecture](./bermond-thomassen-conjecture.md) — Directed graphs with minimum outdegree contain a specific number of disjoint cycles.
* 🔴 [Blankinship-Oporowski Conjecture](./blankinship-oporowski-conjecture.md) — Bounding the book thickness of specific minor closed graph classes.
* 🟢 [Bollobás-Eldridge-Catlin Conjecture](./bollobas-eldridge-catlin-conjecture.md) — Graphs can be partitioned into specific dense subgraphs.
* 🔴 [Bollobás-Riordan Conjecture](./bollobas-riordan-conjecture.md) — Defines critical probability for percolation on random graphs with given degree sequences.
* 🔴 [Bondy's Meta-Conjecture](./bondys-meta-conjecture.md) — Conditions ensuring a Hamiltonian cycle also ensure specific cycle lengths.
* 🟡 [Book Thickness of Graphs](./book-thickness-problem.md) — Determine the minimum number of pages required to embed a graph.
* 🔴 [Bouchet's 6-Flow Conjecture](./bouchets-6-flow-conjecture.md) — Every nowhere zero bidirected graph admits a nowhere zero six flow.
* 🔴 [Brouwer's Laplacian Spectrum Conjecture](./brouwers-laplacian-spectrum-conjecture.md) — Binds the sum of largest Laplacian eigenvalues by the number of edges.
* 🔴 [Burning Number Conjecture](./burning-number-conjecture.md) — Bounding the time required to completely burn a connected graph.
* 🟢 [Burr-Erdős Conjecture](./burr-erdos-conjecture.md) — Sparse graphs have linearly bounded Ramsey numbers.
* 🔴 [Buser's Conjecture](./busers-conjecture.md) — Binds the isoperimetric constant of a graph strictly using its spectral gap.
* 🔴 [Caccetta-Häggkvist Conjecture](./caccetta-haggkvist-conjecture.md) — Directed graphs with sufficient out degree must contain a short cycle.
* 🔴 [Cage Problem](./cage-problem.md) — Find the smallest regular graph with a specific girth.
* 🟢 [Erdős-Faber-Lovász Conjecture](./erdos-faber-lovasz-conjecture.md) — The union of k complete graphs of order k is k colorable.
* 🔴 [Erdős-Gallai Path Decomposition Conjecture](./erdos-gallai-conjecture.md) — Every graph can be decomposed into a linear number of simple paths.
* 🔴 [Erdős-Gyárfás Conjecture](./erdos-gyarfas-conjecture.md) — Cubic graphs contain cycles whose length is a power of two.
* 🟡 [Erdős-Hajnal Conjecture](./erdos-hajnal-conjecture.md) — Graphs without a specific induced subgraph have large homogeneous subsets.
* 🟢 [Erdős-Heilbronn Conjecture](./erdos-heilbronn-conjecture.md) — Binds the cardinality of restricted sumsets of elements in finite abelian groups.
* 🔴 [Erdős Intersection Pattern Problem](./erdos-intersection-pattern-problem.md) — Classifying impossible intersection sizes for families of finite sets.
* 🟢 [Erdős-Ko-Rado Theorem q-analogue](./erdos-ko-rado-q-analogue.md) — Bounding the size of intersecting families of vector spaces.
* 🔴 [Erdős-Lovász Tihany Conjecture](./erdos-lovasz-tihany-conjecture.md) — Splitting a graph into two subgraphs with specific chromatic properties.
* 🟢 [Erdős-Moser Problem](./erdos-moser-problem.md) — Bounding the number of equal distances in a finite point set.
* 🔴 [Erdős-Nešetřil Conjecture](./erdos-nesetril-conjecture.md) — Bounding the strong chromatic index of a graph by a quadratic function.
* 🟡 [Erdős-Rothschild Problem](./erdos-rothschild-problem.md) — Determines the maximum number of edge colorings of graphs avoiding monochromatic cliques.
* 🟡 [Erdős-Rubin-Taylor Conjecture](./erdos-rubin-taylor-conjecture.md) — The choice number of a graph relates to its average degree.
* 🔴 [Erdős-Sós Conjecture](./erdos-sos-conjecture.md) — Graphs with enough edges contain all trees of a specific size.
* 🔴 [Erdős-Szekeres Conjecture](./erdos-szekeres-conjecture.md) — Determine the number of points ensuring a convex polygon.
* 🔴 [Erdős-Turán Conjecture on Additive Bases](./erdos-turan-additive-bases-conjecture.md) — Additive bases must have representation functions that are unbounded.
* 🔴 [Fano Plane Coloring Conjecture](./fano-plane-coloring-conjecture.md) — Colorings of the Fano plane under specific structural restrictions.
* 🔴 [Firefighting on Trees](./firefighting-on-trees.md) — Determine the optimal strategy to stop fire spreading on graphs.
* 🔴 [Forcing Conjecture](./forcing-conjecture.md) — Perfect matchings in bipartite graphs can be uniquely forced.
* 🟢 [Frankl-Rödl Conjecture](./frankl-rodl-conjecture.md) — Bounding the number of intersections in a uniform hypergraph.
* 🟡 [Frankl's Union-Closed Sets Conjecture](./frankls-conjecture.md) — Union closed set families have an element in half the sets.
* 🔴 [Fulkerson's Conjecture](./fulkersons-conjecture.md) — Cubic bridgeless graphs have six perfect matchings covering all edges.
* 🔴 [Graceful Tree Conjecture](./graceful-tree-conjecture.md) — Every tree admits a graceful labeling of its vertices.
* 🟡 [Graph Isomorphism Problem](./graph-isomorphism-problem.md) — Determine if two finite graphs are structurally identical in polynomial time.
* 🟢 [Graph Minor Theorem](./graph-minor-theorem.md) — Every minor closed graph property is characterized by finite forbidden minors.
* 🔴 [Graph Reconstruction Conjecture](./graph-reconstruction-conjecture.md) — Graphs are determined uniquely by their vertex deleted subgraphs.
